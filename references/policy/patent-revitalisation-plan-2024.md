# 2024 plan for revitalising university and institute patents

## What it is

The national working plan for inventorying and commercialising unused patents of universities and research institutes. **No local copy was saved.**

## What the docs take from it

- Patents are inventoried and tagged with a conversion intent (transfer, licence, self-use, industrialisation) in a resource pool; enterprises evaluate them and give feedback (09 §5).
- Licence structures such as entry fee plus royalties or instalments; open licensing for widely applicable patents (research notes).
