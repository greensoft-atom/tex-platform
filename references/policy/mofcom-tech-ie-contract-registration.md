# MOFCOM technology import/export contract registration measures (2009)

## What it is

MOFCOM's measures on registering free-category technology import and export contracts, as recorded on WIPO Lex. **No local copy (cited from web search).**

## What the docs take from it

- Registration within **60 days** after the contract takes effect; royalty-based contracts count from the first determination of the royalty base; restricted technologies follow a licensing path (08, cross-border registration section).
