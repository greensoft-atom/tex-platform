# STCSM 2024 Shanghai Science and Technology Progress Report

## What it is

STCSM's annual progress report (2024 edition, part 4 covering technology transfer and markets). It is a government report rather than a normative document, so only short excerpts are kept here.

## What the docs take from it

- STEX 2024: **2,383** technologies entered, **¥30.933 billion** on-exchange value; cumulative **> 12,800** technologies and **> ¥63.4 billion** (01 §1.4; 02 §2.1).
- "科技成果＋认股权" product launched, first national deal completed (01 §1.2; 09 §2.2).
- NETC 2024: signed deals **近42亿元** (≈ ¥4.2 billion); cumulative **近400亿元** (≈ ¥40 billion); **44** domestic outlets, **11** international channels (01 §2.3; 02 §3; 09 §3.1; README corrections).
- InnoMatch, **cumulative** to end-2024: **6,468** demands, **4,390** results, **3,015** solutions, **313** completed transactions, **¥778 million** landed; in 2024: 595 matching intentions, > ¥3.4 billion intended (01 §2.3; 02 §3 and "Corrections"; 04 §2).
- EXPO model **"1 + 3 + 365"** (09 §3.6).
- Shanghai registered technology contracts 2024: **53,864**, **5,200.73亿元** (01 §4).

## Excerpts

1. STEX
   > 上海技术交易所全年进场成果2383项，进场交易额309.33亿元，累计进场成果超12800个，累计成交金额超634亿元；

   *Translation:* "STEX: 2,383 results entered the exchange during the year, on-exchange transaction value ¥30.933 billion; cumulatively more than 12,800 results and more than ¥63.4 billion transacted;"

2. 科技成果＋认股权
   > “ 科技成果＋认股权”创新产品对外发布并完成全国首单交易案例

   *Translation:* "The innovative 'S&T achievement + equity warrant' product was released and the first transaction case in China was completed."

3. NETC (two-column page; the four lines of the NETC column, in reading order)
   > 国家技术转移东部中心全年布局国内外合作
   >
   > 渠道8家，促成交易签约金额近42亿元，累计
   >
   > 布局国内网点44家，国际合作渠道11家，促
   >
   > 成各类技术交易签约近400亿元

   *Translation:* "NETC added 8 domestic and international cooperation channels during the year and facilitated signed transactions of nearly ¥4.2 billion; cumulatively it has 44 domestic outlets and 11 international cooperation channels and has facilitated technology transaction signings of nearly ¥40 billion."

4. InnoMatch platform (two lines of the InnoMatch block)
   > 达成需求对接意向595项，意向签约额超过34亿元，累计汇聚6468项企业需求、
   >
   > 4390项科技成果，收到3015条解决方案，313项完成交易、落地交易额7.78亿元

   *Translation:* "595 demand-matching intentions reached, intended signings above ¥3.4 billion; cumulatively 6,468 enterprise demands and 4,390 S&T results gathered, 3,015 solutions received, 313 transactions completed with ¥778 million landed."

5. EXPO model
   > 打造“1＋3＋365”展会对接模式，1个InnoMatch全球供需对接平台在线服务全生

   *Translation:* "built the '1 + 3 + 365' exhibition matching model: one InnoMatch global supply-demand platform serving the whole ecosystem online, [3 days of exhibition, 365 days of matching]"

6. Shanghai technology contracts
   > 全年上海技术合同认定登记53864项，成交金额5200.73亿元，分别比上年增长6.0%、7.2%。

   *Translation:* "Shanghai registered 53,864 technology contracts in the year with a value of ¥520.073 billion, up 6.0% and 7.2% year on year."

## Notes

- **Corrected during the parallel revision:** an earlier 01 §2.3 said "11 international cooperation channels (8 added in 2024)". The report says 8 **domestic and international** channels were added ("全年布局国内外合作渠道8家"); the current 01 §2.3 says "8 new domestic and international channels added in 2024".
- The "科技成果＋认股权" line sits under the heading "建设国家级技术交易场所" directly after the STEX sentence; the sentence itself does not name STEX. 09 §2.2 states this placement correctly ("Listed under the national trading venue (STEX)").
- The text extraction interleaves two columns (NETC and Green Technology Bank); the NETC excerpt above joins the NETC column lines in order.
