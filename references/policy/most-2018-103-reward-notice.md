# 国科发政〔2018〕103号 — Cash-reward notice rules

## What it is

MOST's normative notice on publicising cash rewards for service inventions (needed for the related individual-income-tax treatment). Cited by number in 09 §5; **no local copy was saved**.

## What the docs take from it

- The cash-reward notice runs **≥ 15 working days** and includes the **technology-contract registration information** (09 §5).

## Excerpts

None stored (no saved copy). The WebFetch summary quotes item 3 ("公示信息应当包含科技成果转化信息、奖励人员信息、现金奖励信息、技术合同登记信息、公示期限等内容。") and item 5 ("公示期限不得低于15个工作日。"); model-reported.

## Notes

- 09 §5 writes the number as "国科发〔2018〕103号"; the MOST page and research notes give "国科发政〔2018〕103号". The Fudan notice itself writes "国科发【2018】103 号".
