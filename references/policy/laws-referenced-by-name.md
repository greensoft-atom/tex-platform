# Laws referenced by name in doc 08

## What it is

Doc 08 lists statutes by name without links. **No local copies.** They are official texts and could be added to `official-texts/` later from npc.gov.cn or gov.cn.

## What the docs take from them

| Law (as named in 08) | Point used in 08 |
| --- | --- |
| E-Commerce Law (2018) | Platform rules; keep transaction information ≥ 3 years after completion (Art. 31) (08 §1.1; "Data retention") |
| Civil Code (2020), Book III Ch. 20 | Technology development, transfer, licensing, consulting and service contracts are distinct types (08 §1.1; §3) |
| Patent Law (2020 amendment) | Art. 10: assignment needs a written contract registered with CNIPA and takes effect on registration (08 §1.1); open licensing Arts. 50–51 (09 §5) |
| Law on Promoting the Transformation of S&T Achievements (2015) | Art. 18 pricing methods with public notice; Art. 45 default rewards (08 §1.1; §5.4) |
| Regulations on Technology Import and Export Administration; Catalogue of Technologies Prohibited or Restricted from Export (2023) | Prohibited / restricted / free categories; licensing and registration (08 §1.1; §5.3) |
| Export Control Law (2020) | Controlled items in cross-border deals (08 §5.3) |
| Electronic Signature Law | Reliable e-signatures equal handwritten ones (08 §1.1) |
| Cybersecurity Law; Data Security Law; Personal Information Protection Law | Security and personal-information duties (08 §1.1 and checklists) |
| Provisions on Promoting and Regulating Cross-border Data Flows (CAC, 2024) | Cross-border data transfer (08 §5.3) |
| VAT Law (effective 2026) | VAT treatment of technology contracts (08 §5.5) |
