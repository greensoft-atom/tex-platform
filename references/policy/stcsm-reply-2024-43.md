# 沪科建复〔2024〕43号 — STCSM reply on InnoMatch (June 2024)

## What it is

STCSM's published reply to a Shanghai People's Congress deputy's suggestion, describing the InnoMatch platform's results and mechanisms. Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/stcsm-reply-2024-hukejianfu-43.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- Since the August 2022 launch: **4,395** demands, **3,295** results, 1,982 solutions, match rate **> 60 %**, 1,408 intended matches, **> ¥5.239 billion** intended signing (09 §3.1).
- Demand "technology portraits" and a **self-built smart matching tool** with second-level response (09 §3.7).
- About **60 large firms** publishing demands; technology managers took part **18,569** times; **302** service institutions; collaboration rewards > ¥1.5 million (09 §3.1, §3.2).

## Excerpts

1. Platform results
   > 自 2022 年平台 8 月发布以来，累计汇聚企业需求 4395 项、科技成果 3295 项，投递解决方案 1982 条，对接率超过 60%，达成对接意向 1408 项，意向签约额超过 52.39 亿元。

   *Translation:* "Since the platform's launch in August 2022 it has gathered 4,395 enterprise demands and 3,295 S&T results, received 1,982 solutions, achieved a match rate above 60%, reached 1,408 matching intentions and more than ¥5.239 billion of intended signings."

2. Matching technology
   > 平台可针对创新需求技术画像，形成区域与产业交叉的人才模型、产业模型，基于自主研发智能供需匹配工具，通过智能算法全球匹配对接解决方案，响应速度达秒级。

   *Translation:* "The platform builds technology portraits of innovation demands, forms talent and industry models crossing regions and industries, and, with a self-developed smart supply-demand matching tool, matches solutions worldwide by algorithm with response times of seconds."

3. Technology managers and institutions
   > 平台技术经理人参与供需对接 18569 人次，302 家服务机构参与其中，发放协作奖励超 150 万元

   *Translation:* "Technology managers took part in supply-demand matching 18,569 times, 302 service institutions participated, and more than ¥1.5 million of collaboration rewards were paid"

## Notes

- 09 §3.1 writes "mid-2024"; the reply is dated 5 June 2024 and says "累计" (cumulative since August 2022).
