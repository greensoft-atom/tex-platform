# Shanghai due-diligence exemption guideline (2023)

## What it is

A Shanghai guideline for units in the city's S&T-achievement transformation reform pilot (试点单位), defining when managers are not held liable for decision errors. It appears as Annex 1 of the pilot documents (the related STCSM pilot notice 沪科〔2023〕215号 is in `raw/policy/stcsm-tt-reform-pilot-2023-huke-215/`). Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/sh-diligence-exemption-guideline-2023.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- No liability for later value changes when the price was set by **listing or auction on a technology market**, or when a **negotiated price was publicly notified both in the unit and on a technology trading market** (09 Summary item 5; 09 §5; 08 §5.4).
- A negative list of results that may not be granted to researchers (赋权) (research notes; 09 §5 grant row context).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 6 (4) — pricing-based exemption
   > （四）管理人员和领导人员通过技术交易市场挂牌交易、拍卖等方式确定价格，或者通过协议定价并在本单位及技术交易市场公示拟交易价格，但科技成果后续产生较大的价值变化，导致试点单位利益受损的。

   *Translation:* "(4) where managers and leaders set the price by listing or auction on a technology trading market, or by negotiated pricing with the proposed price publicly notified in the unit and on a technology trading market, but the result later changes significantly in value and the pilot unit suffers a loss."

2. Art. 6 — consequence
   > 符合以下情形之一的，不予追究相关人员决策失误责任。

   *Translation:* "In any of the following situations, the persons concerned are not held liable for decision-making errors."

## Notes

- **Scope:** the guideline is written for **pilot units** ("试点单位") and exempts **managers and leaders** from **decision-error** liability. An earlier 09 Summary item 5 said dual publication "exempts staff from due-diligence liability" without that scope; the current 09 §5 and 08 §5.4 state the pilot scope ("Written for the city's reform-pilot units; other public research units may follow it"), and the current Summary item 5 is softened ("lets staff rely on the due-diligence exemption").
- Interpreting bodies and term: Art. 9 ("本指引由上海市科学技术委员会、上海市教育委员会、上海市卫生健康委员会予以解释") and Art. 10 ("本指引在实施方案有效期内施行").
