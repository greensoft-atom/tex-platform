# 沪府规〔2025〕2号 — Shanghai trading venue measures (2025)

## What it is

The Shanghai government's general rules for all licensed trading venues (commodity spot, equity-type and other contract trading), the parent document of STCSM's technology-venue rules [policy/sh-venue-rules-2025](sh-venue-rules-2025.md). Art. 4 names the science and technology department as supervisor of technology venues such as STEX. Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/sh-trading-venue-measures-2025-hufugui-2.txt).

## What the docs take from it

Points for D-01 and the fee questions:

- **Members, agents and authorised service institutions are regulated like branches** (Art. 2), and a venue may in principle **not** operate through members, agents or authorised service institutions unless the provincial and Shanghai governments approve (Art. 11). Whether STEX's "交易服务机构" (practising agents admitted under rule 105) count as such and are covered by an approval is **not stated anywhere found**; this is a legal-validation item for D-01.
- A venue's trading rules must state **the fee standard and how fees are collected** (Art. 22); changes to trading rules need the supervisor's consent (Art. 12).
- The list of members, agents and authorised service institutions must be published on the venue's website (Art. 25).
- Venues and their members, agents and service institutions must not, among other things, act without client instruction, misappropriate client funds, or **induce unnecessary trades to earn commissions** (Art. 26) — relevant to how an agent platform presents and charges.
- Third-party settlement of trading funds through a bank or non-bank payment institution, per-client sub-accounts (Art. 21; repeated in 沪科规〔2025〕2号 Art. 12).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 2 (3rd para.) and Art. 11 — agents treated as branches
   > 交易场所的会员、代理商、授权服务机构等，按照分支机构管理。

   *Translation:* "A trading venue's members, agents, authorised service institutions and the like are managed as branches."

   > 第十一条 交易场所原则上不得设立分支机构及通过发展会员、代理商、授权服务机构等开展经营活动。确有必要的，应当分别经该交易场所所在地省级政府和上海市政府批准。

   *Translation:* "Art. 11. In principle a trading venue shall not set up branches or conduct business by developing members, agents, authorised service institutions and the like. Where truly necessary, this must be approved by the provincial government of the venue's location and by the Shanghai government."

2. Art. 22 — fee standard in the trading rules
   > 交易规则内容包括交易品种和交割（交收）期限，交易方式和流程，风险管理，交易标的交割（交收）、资金清算规则，交易纠纷解决机制，交易费用标准和收取方式，交易信息的处理和发布规则，其他异常处理、差错处理机制等事项。

   *Translation:* "The trading rules cover the trading products and delivery periods, trading methods and process, risk management, delivery and fund-clearing rules, the dispute-resolution mechanism, **the trading fee standard and the way fees are collected**, rules for processing and publishing trading information, and other exception- and error-handling mechanisms."

3. Art. 25 (3rd para.) — public list of agents
   > 交易场所分支机构、会员、代理商、授权服务机构名单与相关信息应当在营业场所及网站显著位置进行公示。

   *Translation:* "The list of a trading venue's branches, members, agents and authorised service institutions, with related information, shall be displayed prominently at its business premises and on its website."

4. Art. 26 (5) — commissions
   > （五）为牟取佣金收入，诱使客户进行不必要的交易；

   *Translation:* "(5) inducing clients to make unnecessary trades in order to earn commission income;"
