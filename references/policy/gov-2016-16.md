# 国发〔2016〕16号 — State Council provisions on transforming S&T achievements

## What it is

The State Council's implementing provisions for the 2015 Law on Promoting the Transformation of S&T Achievements. An official document whose text could be committed, but **no local copy was saved** (only a WebFetch summary), so it is not in `official-texts/`.

## What the docs take from it

- Prices by negotiation, listing on a technology market or auction; for negotiated prices, the result name and proposed price are publicly notified in the unit **for at least 15 days**, with a published objection procedure (09 §5; 08 §5.4).
- National minimum of **50 %** of net income for inventor rewards (09 §5 "Inventor rewards").
- Leaders exempt from decision liability for later value changes if diligent (context for 09 §5; 08 §5.4).
- Annual transformation report due 30 March (research notes; 09 §6 reporting exports).
