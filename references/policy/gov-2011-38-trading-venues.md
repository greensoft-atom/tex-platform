# 国发〔2011〕38号 — State Council decision on trading-venue clean-up

## What it is

The State Council decision that set the national clean-up regime for trading venues (交易场所), on which Shanghai's venue rules rest. **No local copy of the decision itself.** The local raw files are STCSM's 2020 drafting explanation for the Shanghai venue rules, which cites this decision as the background.

## What the docs take from it

- A venue using "交易所" in its name needs approval; unapproved venues may not split rights into equal shares for public sale or trade them by centralized bidding or market making (08 §1.1). Hence: do not call the platform an exchange; no binding bidding on the platform (08 §1.1, decision D-03).

## Excerpts

1. 2020 drafting explanation (STCSM) — background (quoted from the local draft explanation, not from the decision)
   > 根据国务院《关于清理整顿各类交易场所切实防范金融风险的决定》

   *Translation:* "In accordance with the State Council's Decision on Cleaning Up and Rectifying Trading Venues to Prevent Financial Risks"

   > 各省级人民政府应审慎批设交易场所，制定本地区监管制度。

   *Translation:* "provincial governments should approve trading venues prudently and set up local supervisory systems."

## Notes

- The specific prohibitions summarised in 08 §1.1 (equal-share splitting, centralized bidding, market making) were not verified against the decision text in this research.
