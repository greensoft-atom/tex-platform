# Technology-contract registration measures (2000)

## What it is

The 2000 measures on recognition and registration of technology contracts, since replaced by revised measures effective 1 March 2026 (see [CLP](clp-miit-registration-2026.md), [Ecovis](ecovis-registration-2026.md), [yzwb](yzwb-2026-08.md)). **No local copy was saved.**

## What the docs take from it

- The selling or performing party registers once, by locality, after the contract is formed; written contracts only; the registration body decides **within 30 days** and certifies the technical-income amount; registration is the gateway to tax preferences (09 §5).
