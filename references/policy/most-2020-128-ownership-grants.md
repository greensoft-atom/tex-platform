# 国科发区〔2020〕128号 — Ownership and long-term use rights for researchers

## What it is

The national pilot plan allowing universities and institutes to grant researchers ownership (co-ownership) or a long-term use right of service inventions. An official document, but **no local copy was saved**.

## What the docs take from it

- Co-ownership or a **long-term use right of ≥ 10 years**; team agreement → application → approval → notice **≥ 15 days** → written agreement; results affecting national security and similar interests are excluded (negative list) (09 §5).
