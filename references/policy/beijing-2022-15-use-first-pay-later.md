# 京科发〔2022〕15号 — Beijing "use first, pay later" pilot

## What it is

Beijing's pilot plan letting universities, institutes and hospitals license results to SMEs with deferred payment. Cited by number in 09 §5; **no local copy was saved**.

## What the docs take from it

- Licensees: **SMEs only** (MIIT SME classification); payment **at least one year after the licence takes effect or after the licensee earns revenue** from the result; structures "零门槛费+里程碑支付+收入提成" or deferred licence fees (09 §5).
