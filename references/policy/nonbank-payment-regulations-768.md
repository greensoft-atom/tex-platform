# State Council Decree No. 768 — Non-bank payment institutions

## What it is

The State Council regulation on non-bank payment institutions. **No local copy (cited from web search).** The cited PDF is a copy on a municipal government site; the authoritative text is on gov.cn / PBOC.

## What the docs take from it

- Published December 2023, in force **1 May 2024**; payment business only by institutions licensed by the People's Bank of China; regulated payment processing and customer reserve funds; no misappropriation of reserve funds (08 Summary; 08 §1.1).
- Consequence used in 08 and 09: the platform must not hold deposits or prices in its own accounts.

## Excerpts

None (no local copy). The search-result summary states the decree was signed on 9 December 2023, takes effect on 1 May 2024, has 6 chapters and 60 articles, and requires PBOC approval and a payment-business licence.
