# 沪科规〔2025〕2号 — Shanghai technology trading venue rules (2025)

## What it is

STCSM's normative document governing licensed technology trading venues (技术交易场所) in Shanghai, such as STEX: approval, changes, exit, duties, settlement, evidence value of certificates, conduct and reporting. Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/sh-venue-rules-2025-hukegui-2.txt). Earlier drafts (2020 draft and explanation; 2025 revision draft for comment) are kept in `raw/policy/` without separate notes.

## What the docs take from it

- Trading methods: 协议转让, 拍卖, 招标, 竞价; modes: transfer, licence, 作价投资, development, service, consulting (Art. 2) (01 §1.3).
- New venues under "总量控制、合理布局、审慎审批"; no trading while preparing (Art. 6) (01 §1.3; 08 Summary; 09 §1).
- Changes to products, modes, rules or major shareholders need STCSM consent; senior-manager changes filed within 10 WD (Arts. 7–8) (09 §1).
- Venue duties: rules, rights registration and transaction certification, disclosure including university notices, valuation, settlement, service-institution management, mediation, suitability, internal controls (Art. 11) (01 §1.3; 09 §1).
- **Third-party settlement** through a bank or non-bank payment institution, segregated per client (Art. 12) (01 §1.3; 08 Summary; 09 §1).
- Certificates as supporting evidence for transfer, diligence, negotiated pricing, rewards, judicial appraisal, contract certification (Art. 13) (01 §1.3; 09 §1).
- Recusal and no improper benefit (Art. 15); monthly, quarterly and annual reports (Art. 16) (01 §1.3; 09 §1).
- Validity 1 May 2025 – 30 April 2030 (Art. 22) (01 §1.1; 09 §1).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 2 — methods
   > 技术交易可以采取协议转让、拍卖、招标、竞价和符合国家政策法规的其他方式。

   *Translation:* "Technology transactions may use negotiated transfer, auction, tender, bidding and other methods consistent with national policy and law."

2. Art. 6 — approval principle
   > 按照“总量控制、合理布局、审慎审批”的原则，依规设立技术交易场所。

   *Translation:* "Technology trading venues are established according to the principles of 'total-quantity control, rational layout and prudent approval'."

3. Art. 12 — third-party settlement
   > 实行交易资金第三方结算制度，由交易资金的开户银行或非银行支付机构负责交易资金的结算，按客户实行分账管理

   *Translation:* "apply a third-party settlement system for transaction funds, with settlement handled by the bank holding the account or a non-bank payment institution, and segregated accounts per client"

4. Art. 13 — evidence value
   > 技术交易场所出具的技术权益登记凭证、技术交易凭证、公示证明等，应依据相关法律法规，可作为科技成果产权转移、市场主体勤勉尽责情况判定、科技成果协议定价、科技成果转化现金或股权奖励实施、相关司法鉴定开展、技术投融资决策、技术合同认定等方面的辅助参考依据

   *Translation:* "Rights-registration certificates, transaction certificates and notice certificates issued by a venue may, under the relevant laws, serve as supporting reference for transfer of ownership of S&T results, judging whether market actors exercised due diligence, negotiated pricing, cash or equity rewards, judicial appraisal, technology investment decisions and technology-contract certification"

5. Art. 22 — validity
   > 本细则自 2025 年 5 月 1 日起实施，有效期至2030 年 4 月 30 日。

   *Translation:* "These rules take effect on 1 May 2025 and remain valid until 30 April 2030."

## Notes

- **Quote (corrected during the parallel revision):** earlier versions of 09 gave the approval principle as "总量控制、审慎审批"; the text is "总量控制、合理布局、审慎审批" (Art. 6), which the current 01 §1.3 and 09 Summary now quote in full.
- **Interpretation, not text:** 08 Summary item 1 and 09 Summary item 1 say formal listing, bidding, settlement and certificates "are venue functions". Art. 11 lists them as duties a venue must perform; the rules do not themselves say that non-venues are barred from them. The exclusivity follows from the general trading-venue clean-up regime (the 2020 draft explanation cites 国发〔2011〕38号), which is a legal-validation item.
