# 国办发明电〔2025〕7号 — 2026 public holidays and make-up working days

## What it is

The State Council General Office's annual notice fixing the 2026 public holidays and the weekend days that become working days (调休上班). It is the official source for the working-day calendar that every WD timer in the docs relies on (FR-ADM-09). Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/gov-holidays-2026-guobanfamingdian-2025-7.txt).

## What the docs take from it

The 2026 calendar data for FR-ADM-09:

| Holiday | Days off (inclusive) | Length | Make-up working days (调休上班) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New Year (元旦) | 1–3 Jan (Thu–Sat) | 3 | Sun 4 Jan |
| Spring Festival (春节) | 15–23 Feb (Sun–Mon) | 9 | Sat 14 Feb, Sat 28 Feb |
| Qingming (清明节) | 4–6 Apr (Sat–Mon) | 3 | — |
| Labour Day (劳动节) | 1–5 May (Fri–Tue) | 5 | Sat 9 May |
| Dragon Boat (端午节) | 19–21 Jun (Fri–Sun) | 3 | — |
| Mid-Autumn (中秋节) | 25–27 Sep (Fri–Sun) | 3 | — |
| National Day (国庆节) | 1–7 Oct (Thu–Wed) | 7 | Sun 20 Sep, Sat 10 Oct |

- Six make-up working days in 2026: 4 Jan, 14 Feb, 28 Feb, 9 May, 20 Sep, 10 Oct.
- The **2027** notice had not been published when this was checked (2026-10-19); the notice for a year is usually issued in November of the year before (the 2025 notice on 2024-11-12, the 2026 notice on 2025-11-04). FR-ADM-09 data for 2027 must be entered when it appears.

## Excerpts

1. Spring Festival
   > 二、春节：2月15日（农历腊月二十八、周日）至23日（农历正月初七、周一）放假调休，共9天。2月14日（周六）、2月28日（周六）上班。

   *Translation:* "2. Spring Festival: 15 February (Sunday, 28th day of the 12th lunar month) to 23 February (Monday, 7th day of the 1st lunar month) off with adjusted rest days, 9 days in total. Work on 14 February (Saturday) and 28 February (Saturday)."

2. Labour Day and National Day
   > 四、劳动节：5月1日（周五）至5日（周二）放假调休，共5天。5月9日（周六）上班。

   *Translation:* "4. Labour Day: 1 May (Friday) to 5 May (Tuesday) off with adjusted rest days, 5 days in total. Work on 9 May (Saturday)."

   > 七、国庆节：10月1日（周四）至7日（周三）放假调休，共7天。9月20日（周日）、10月10日（周六）上班。

   *Translation:* "7. National Day: 1 October (Thursday) to 7 October (Wednesday) off with adjusted rest days, 7 days in total. Work on 20 September (Sunday) and 10 October (Saturday)."

3. New Year
   > 一、元旦：1月1日（周四）至3日（周六）放假调休，共3天。1月4日（周日）上班。

   *Translation:* "1. New Year's Day: 1 January (Thursday) to 3 January (Saturday) off with adjusted rest days, 3 days in total. Work on 4 January (Sunday)."

## Notes

- The notice is a 明电 (open telegram) and has no articles; the numbered items are quoted above. Qingming, Dragon Boat and Mid-Autumn have no make-up day.
- Venues may also publish their own closing notices (STEX published one for Labour Day 2024, `stte.com/articles/1325`, seen only as a link title); the platform calendar should follow the State Council notice and allow venue-specific closures as overrides.
