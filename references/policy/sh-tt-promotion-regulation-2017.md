# 上海市促进科技成果转化条例 (2017)

## What it is

Shanghai's local regulation on the transformation of S&T achievements, as adopted in 2017. The copy is a Word file on a university website; the content is the official regulation. Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/sh-tt-promotion-regulation-2017.txt). Later amendments, if any, were not checked.

## What the docs take from it

- Units may set inventor rewards of **at least 70 %** of net transfer or licence income (Art. 34) (09 §5; 08 §5.4).
- Pricing by negotiation, listing on a technology market or auction (Art. 9) (context for 09 §5 and 08 §5.4).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 34 (1) — 70 % rule
   > （一）将职务科技成果转让、许可给他人实施的，可以从该项科技成果转让净收入或者许可净收入中提取不低于百分之七十的比例；

   *Translation:* "(1) where a service invention is transferred or licensed to others, [the unit] may allocate not less than 70% of the net transfer or licence income;"

2. Art. 9 — pricing methods
   > 研发机构、高等院校应当依法通过协议定价、在技术交易市场挂牌交易、拍卖等方式，确定科技成果转让、许可或者作价投资的价格。

   *Translation:* "R&D institutions and universities shall, according to law, set the price of transfers, licences or equity investments of S&T results by negotiated pricing, listing on a technology trading market, auction or similar methods."

## Notes

- Art. 34 is permissive ("允许…规定或者与科技人员约定" … "可以…提取不低于百分之七十"): it allows units to set at least 70 % by rule or agreement. The current 08 §5.4 and 09 §5 state it this way (an earlier 09 shorthand "Shanghai ≥ 70%" read as a mandatory floor).
