# 沪科建复〔2025〕38号 — STCSM reply on university technology markets (June 2025)

## What it is

STCSM's published reply (with the Education Commission) to a People's Congress deputy's suggestion on university technology markets. It reports InnoMatch and STEX figures as of mid-2025. Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/stcsm-reply-2025-hukejianfu-38.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- InnoMatch: registered users **近 3 万** (≈ 30,000) in 36 countries and regions, **> 6,600** demands, **> 4,600** results, **> 3,000** solutions, **近 8 亿元** (≈ ¥800 million) signed, match rate ≈ 65 % (09 §3.1).
- STEX: "model evaluation + expert price inquiry + market price"; **16** evaluation products; **> 30,000** patents graded; **> 1,200** results evaluated; cumulative transactions **> ¥65.3 billion** and **> 13,000** results (09 §2.2).

## Excerpts

1. InnoMatch
   > 目前注册用户已近 3 万人，遍布全球 36 个国家和地区，累计征集企业需求超 6600 项，汇集成果超 4600 项，收到解决方案超 3000 个，形成实际落地签约金额近 8 亿元，对接率近 65%。

   *Translation:* "It now has nearly 30,000 registered users in 36 countries and regions, has collected more than 6,600 enterprise demands and more than 4,600 results, received more than 3,000 solutions, achieved nearly ¥800 million of signed deals actually implemented, and a match rate of nearly 65%."

2. STEX evaluation products
   > 形成专利申请前评估、专利分级盘点等 16 项评价服务产品。

   *Translation:* "…has developed 16 evaluation service products, such as pre-filing patent assessment and patent grading."

   > 目前上海技术交易所已完成技术交易金额超 653 亿元，进场交易成果超 13000 项，合作专业机构超 100 家，超 3 万项专利完成分级分类，超 1200 项成果完成评价。

   *Translation:* "STEX has so far completed technology transactions of more than ¥65.3 billion, with more than 13,000 results traded on the exchange, more than 100 partner professional institutions, more than 30,000 patents graded and classified, and more than 1,200 results evaluated."

## Notes

- InnoMatch figures here are cumulative since 2022. An earlier 09 §3.1 row was labelled "InnoMatch, 2025"; the current 01 §2.3 and 09 §3.1 say "cumulative to June 2025".
