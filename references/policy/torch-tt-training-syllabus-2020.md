# Torch Center training syllabus for technology-transfer professionals (2020)

## What it is

The national training syllabus for junior technology brokers, mid-level technology brokers and senior technology managers, issued by MOST's Torch Center. It is a training syllabus of a public institution rather than a law or regulation, so it is treated here as a non-official document (excerpts only).

## What the docs take from it

- Course hours: junior broker **24 h**, mid-level **40 h**, senior technology manager **56 h**, plus optional 10-hour capability modules; written exams and case reports (09 §5).

## Excerpts

1. Junior level
   > 政策法规模块、实务技能模块一采用课堂授课，共 24 学时。

   *Translation:* "The policy-and-regulation module and practical-skills module 1 are taught in class, 24 class hours in total."

2. Mid level
   > 为 40 学时。其中，初级技术经纪人所需课程模块 24 学时

   *Translation:* "…totals 40 class hours, of which the modules required for junior technology brokers are 24 class hours"

3. Senior level
   > 块、实务技能模块一、实务技能模块二和实务技能模块三，总学时为 56

   *Translation:* "…[knowledge, policy] modules, practical-skills modules 1, 2 and 3; total class hours 56"
