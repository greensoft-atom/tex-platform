# 国知发运字〔2025〕16号 — CNIPA patent commercialisation notice (2025)

## What it is

CNIPA's 2025 notice on making patent commercialisation a long-term mechanism. Cited by number in 09 §5; **no local copy was saved**.

## What the docs take from it

- Universities should move from one-off buyouts to **"先使用后付费"**: zero upfront fee ("零门槛费"), then instalments, milestones or royalties (09 §5).
