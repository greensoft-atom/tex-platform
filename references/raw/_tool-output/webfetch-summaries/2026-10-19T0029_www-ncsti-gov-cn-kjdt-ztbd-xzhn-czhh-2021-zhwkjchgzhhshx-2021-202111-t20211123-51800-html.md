**2. Procedure, in order**
1. **Application:** The holder (持有方) applies to the trading service institution.
2. **Acceptance:** After review, the institution issues a 《交易信息发布受理通知书》 (acceptance notice) and publishes the listing.
3. **Registration of interested buyers:** During the posting period, the institution registers interested buyers (意向受让方), confirms their eligibility, and gives the holder written notice of the registrations and eligibility opinions.
4. **Bidding and signing:** The institution organizes matching based on the number of interested buyers. Contract signing follows.
5. **Settlement:** The institution sends a transaction confirmation (成交确认书), the buyer pays into the designated account, and the institution issues a payment-received notice. The holder can request transfer of the funds once conditions are met.
6. **Transaction certificate:** Both parties pay the service fee, and the institution issues a 交易凭证 (transaction certificate) after receiving it.
