**Key provisions of the 技术合同认定登记管理办法 (2000)**

- **Who applies (Art. 8):** The applicant is the party providing the technical work or benefit: the research and development party in a development contract, the transferor (让与人) in a transfer contract, the trustee (受托人) in consulting and service contracts, and the trainer or intermediary in training and intermediary contracts. Under Art. 5, entities that pay rewards from technical net income must also register the relevant contracts. The document does not use the term "卖方."
- **When (Art. 8):** Application is made after the contract takes effect ("合同成立后").
- **Where (Art. 8):** Application goes to the technology contract registration body in the applicant's locality. Art. 8 establishes a "按地域一次登记制度" (one-time registration by region).
- **Time limit (Art. 14):** The registration body must finish within 30 days of accepting the application. Under Art. 10, if the contract or attachments are incomplete, the body must request corrections in writing within a set deadline.
- **Materials (Art. 9):** Applicants must submit the complete written contract and related attachments. The model text issued by the Ministry of Science and Technology may be used, or another written contract that complies with the Contract Law. Oral contracts are not accepted.
- **Contract types (Art. 2):** Technology development, transfer, consulting, and service contracts are covered. Training and intermediary contracts may apply voluntarily under the same rules.
- **Fees (not specified):** The document does not set fees for applicants. Art. 19 says the registration body's operating costs follow national rules.
- **Registration certificate (Art. 14):** Approved contracts receive a 技术合同登记证明 stating the verified technical revenue amount ("技术性收入额"). Under Art. 18, changes, transfers, or cancellations require updated registration, and revenue is re-verified for changes.
- **Tax linkage (Arts. 6, 7, 17):** Only registered contracts qualify for state tax, credit, and reward preferences (Art. 6). The certificate holder applies to the competent tax authority, which approves after review (Art. 7). Tax or finance authorities may ask the registration body to re-examine a determination and may refuse a preference application (Art. 17).
- **Not registered (Arts. 6, 9, 11, 14):** Unregistered contracts cannot get the preferences (Art. 6). Oral contracts are rejected (Art. 9). Contracts with non-standard names or unclear nature are returned for correction (Art. 11). Contracts found non-technical or ineligible are returned marked "未予登记" (Art. 14). Parties who submit false information bear responsibility (Art. 12).
