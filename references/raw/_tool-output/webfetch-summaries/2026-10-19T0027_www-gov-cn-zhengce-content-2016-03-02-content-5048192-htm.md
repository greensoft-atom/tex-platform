**1. Pricing methods (Section 一(三))**
- "国家设立的研究开发机构、高等院校对其持有的科技成果，应当通过协议定价、在技术交易市场挂牌交易、拍卖等市场化方式确定价格。"
- Summary: Publicly funded research institutes and universities must set prices through negotiated pricing, listing on a technology trading market, or auction.

**2. Publicity requirements (Sections 一(三) and 十)**
- Negotiated pricing (一(三)): "协议定价的，科技成果持有单位应当在本单位公示科技成果名称和拟交易价格，公示时间不少于15日。"
- Dispute procedures (一(三)): "单位应当明确并公开异议处理程序和办法。"
- Section 十 also refers to publishing the proposed price "在本单位及技术交易市场公示拟交易价格".
- Summary: For negotiated pricing, the holding unit must publish the technology name and proposed price within its own organization for at least 15 days, and must publicly state how objections are handled.

**3. Revenue distribution percentages (Section 六)**
- Technology transfer or licensing (六(1)): "以技术转让或者许可方式转化职务科技成果的，应当从技术转让或者许可所取得的净收入中提取不低于50%的比例用于奖励。"
- Equity or capital contribution (六(2)): "以科技成果作价投资实施转化的，应当从作价投资取得的股份或者出资比例中提取不低于50%的比例用于奖励。"
- Key contributors (六(3)): "在研究开发和科技成果转化中作出主要贡献的人员，获得奖励的份额不低于奖励总额的50%。"

**4. Decision-making exemption (Section 十)**
- "单位领导在履行勤勉尽责义务、没有牟取非法利益的前提下，免除其在科技成果定价中因科技成果转化后续价值变化产生的决策责任。"
- Summary: Unit leaders are exempt from decision liability for later value changes in priced technology, provided they performed their duties diligently and did not seek illegal gain. This applies to pricing done through listing, auction, or publicly disclosed negotiated pricing.

**5. Annual reporting (Section 一(五))**
- Submission to the supervisory authority: "于每年3月30日前向其主管部门报送本单位上一年度科技成果转化情况的年度报告"
- Review and forwarding: "主管部门审核后于每年4月30日前将各单位科技成果转化年度报告报送至科技、财政行政主管部门指定的信息管理系统。"
- Summary: Institutions file the prior year's report by March 30, and the supervisory authority forwards reviewed reports to the designated information system by April 30. Required content includes outcomes, transfers and licensing, industry-academia cooperation, and incentive and reward details.
