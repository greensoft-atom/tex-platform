**Key operational clauses**

- **Inventory scope and cut-off:** Institutions must take stock of all existing patents they were granted by the end of 2023. "2024年及以后授权的专利，及时纳入盘点范围。" Patents granted from 2024 onward are added as they are granted. Invention patents in key industries and advantageous disciplines take priority.

- **Platform:** The "国家专利导航综合服务平台" (National Patent Navigation Comprehensive Service Platform) is the central platform. It is referred to as the 综合服务平台 (comprehensive service platform).

- **Transfer intention categories:** Institutions flag each patent's transfer intention by mode: transfer, licensing, self-use, or industrialization. The results go into a transferable patent resource pool (专利转化资源库).

- **Enterprise evaluation and feedback:** Enterprises in relevant industries assess industrialization prospects and feed back technical improvement needs and cooperation intent. "由这些企业对专利转化资源库中的专利能否真正实现产业化进行评价，并反馈相关需求" Results are entered on the platform or reported through provincial IP bureaus.

- **Tiering criteria:** The page does not give explicit tier definitions. It says high-value patents get matched resources, and that different types of transferable patents are handled with differentiated measures.

- **Promotion measures:**
  - For high-value patents, local industrial, investment, and financing policies are coordinated. These are matched with investor connections, patent pledge financing, insurance, and IPO-related IP services.
  - Institutions are encouraged to use ordinary licensing for high-value patents. "倡导高校和科研机构根据实际情况采取普通许可进行高价值专利的转化"
  - Licensing fees may be structured as an entry fee plus royalties, or as installments. "采取入门费加提成或者分期付款的方式"
  - Open licensing is recommended for widely applicable, practical patents. "对于市场应用范围广泛、实用性较强、适于多地多方实施的专利，指导进行开放许可。"
  - The page does not mention "先使用后付费" (pay-after-use). Licensing to SMEs is covered only generally, through recommended matchmaking with enterprises such as specialized and sophisticated SMEs.

- **Deadlines:**
  - End of March 2024: pilot inventories at one to two universities per province.
  - End of June 2024: national IP pilot and demonstration universities finish full inventory entry. Other institutions reach over 30%.
  - End of 2024: "实现全国高校和科研机构未转化有效专利盘点全覆盖" (full inventory coverage of unconverted valid patents nationwide).
  - End of 2025: "加速转化一批高价值专利" (accelerate conversion of a batch of high-value patents).

- **Statistics and monitoring:** The NIPA monitors progress through the platform and conducts random spot checks. It also periodically compiles data on conversion and industrialization outcomes, which serve as evaluation indicators for local implementation. "定期统计体现高校和科研机构专利转化和产业化成效的相关数据"
