**Trading process (listing transactions for 职务科技成果)**

- **Application (§二.1):** "由持有方向技术交易服务机构提出交易申请；"
- **Information release (§二.2):** "技术交易服务机构审核申请资料通过后，向持有方出具《交易信息发布受理通知书》并发布；" The page does not state the length of the public notice period (公告期) in days. Section §四.3 requires the holder to submit the release application materials and take responsibility for their accuracy.
- **Buyer registration (§二.3):** "在信息发布公告期，技术交易服务机构对有意购买本项目的意向受让方进行登记，" The agency must then inform the holder in writing of the registration results and its eligibility opinion.
- **Eligibility conditions (§四.5):** "持有方可以依照法律法规、批准文件在交易信息发布申请书中设置意向方资格条件；"
- **Selection methods (§二.4; §四.4):** With one bidder, a direct agreement is used: "如征集到一家意向受让方可采用协议成交，" With two or more, the options are "网络竞价、竞争性谈判或综合评议确认受让方". The holder selects the method in the application.
- **Deposit (§四.7):** "受让方应当按照技术交易服务机构的相关规定，支付交易保证金或交易价款，" and the deposit may be converted into the price under the agreement.
- **Deal confirmation (§二.5):** "技术交易服务机构根据技术转让合同约定，向受让方发送成交确认书，"
- **Contract signing (§二.4):** "确定受让方后，组织交易双方签署交易合同；"
- **Fund settlement (§二.5; §四.9):** "受让方向指定结算账户支付交易价款，" and the agency then issues a payment confirmation letter. The holder requests the transfer of funds after the conditions are met.
- **Transaction voucher (§二.6):** "技术交易服务机构收到交易服务费后出具交易凭证，并通知交易双方领取。"
- **Registration of ownership change (§四.8):** Where ownership must be changed, the parties register the change with the relevant authorities using the voucher and related documents.
