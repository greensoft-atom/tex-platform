- **Definition:** "指高等院校、科研机构、医疗卫生机构将科技成果许可给中小微企业使用" (licensing achievements to SMEs by these institutions).

- Small and medium-sized and micro enterprises must meet the criteria in the "《关于印发中小企业划型标准规定的通知》(工信部联企业〔2011〕300号)" (MIIT notice on SME classification standards).
