# WIPO GREEN — FAQs

## What it is

An international reference used in doc 07. **No local copy (cited from the original draft's web search).** The link was checked for availability only (HTTP 200 on 2026-10-19).

## What the docs take from it

- WIPO GREEN connects providers and seekers of green technology: organisations submit technologies and needs, update them and contact partners (07 §1).
- Submissions are reviewed for completeness, clarity and relevance; WIPO does not independently verify every claim (e.g. environmental impact) (07 §1: "A connection platform with light review, not an escrow service").
- Supports licensing, collaboration, joint ventures and sales (07 §1); provider/seeker roles mirrored in 07 §2.1.
