# WIPO — Successful Technology Licensing (Publication No. 903)

## What it is

An international reference used in doc 07. **No local copy (cited from the original draft's web search).** The link was checked for availability only (HTTP 200 on 2026-10-19).

## What the docs take from it

- Licences, assignments, confidentiality agreements and R&D cooperation agreements are distinct technology-transfer instruments; the deal type must be a first-class field (07 §3.3; 07 §7).
- Terms are negotiated for the circumstances, with confidentiality, scope and payment terms (07 §4.1).
- Payment structures: upfront fees, milestone payments, royalties and other negotiated compensation (07 §6).
