# ARENA Commercial Readiness Index (2014)

## What it is

A 16-page ARENA paper defining a six-level Commercial Readiness Index (CRI) that complements TRL. Government agency publication (Australia); excerpts only.

## What the docs take from it

- Commercial readiness as a separate scale, **CRI 1–6** (04 §8.2).
- CRI is distinct from TRL; commercial risk remains after technology risk is retired (04 §8.2 rationale).

## Excerpts

1. Why CRI
   > While the majority of technology risk is retired through the TRL 1–9 framework there is often significant commercial uncertainty and risk remaining in the demonstration and deployment phase.

2. Lowest and highest levels
   > Hypothetical commercial proposition: Technically ready – commercially untested and unproven.

   > "Bankable" grade asset class driven by same criteria as other mature energy technologies.

3. Relation to TRL
   > The figures demonstrate that the CRI begins once the technology is at the stage where there is research to prove that it is feasible in the field (TRL 2).

## Notes

- ARENA itself places TRL and CRI on one technology development chain (Figures 1–2) and starts CRI at TRL 2. 04 §8.2's rule "Do not use a fixed TRL → CRL mapping … a TRL 8 technology can still be at the lowest commercial-readiness level" is consistent with treating them as separate scales, but is the docs' design choice rather than a statement in this paper. The docs write "CRL" in one place and "CRI" in another for the same scale.
