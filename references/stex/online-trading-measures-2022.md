# STEX online and paperless trading measures (2022)

## What it is

A 2022 STEX notice to all market participants listing seven measures to keep trading going during the Shanghai lockdown. It is the most explicit STEX statement found about **how participants interact with its systems**: through the STEX website, the **TECHDEAL app**, and uploads of electronic attachments to the trading system, plus hotline, WeChat and e-mail. It mentions no API, data feed or system-to-system interface.

## What the docs take from it

For the "data interface for agents" question (D-01, FR-VEN):

- Participants (including agents) submit listings and applications by **logging in to the STEX website or the TECHDEAL app** and uploading documents as electronic attachments; acceptance, review and publication run online.
- No programmatic interface is mentioned here or in any other STEX page found; the design should assume **manual entry through the venue's web portal** (bid.stte.com) unless STEX offers an interface on request.

## Excerpts

1. Website and app
   > 1、 引导线上无纸化交易。积极通过上技所交易系统开展“无纸化交易”服务，引导各交易主体通过登陆上技所网站、APP（TECHDEAL）等渠道实施线上无纸化技术交易和信息发布，充分利用信息化、数字化手段服务市场主体、保障业务开展。

   *Translation:* "1. Promote online paperless trading. Use the STEX trading system for 'paperless trading' and guide all trading parties to carry out online paperless technology trading and information publication by logging in to the STEX website, the app (TECHDEAL) and other channels, making full use of digital means to serve market participants."

2. Uploads to the trading system
   > 4.推行无纸化交易受理审核。市场主体可将交易材料以电子附件的形式上传至交易系统，全流程通过线上完成受理、审核流程以及信息发布。

   *Translation:* "4. Paperless acceptance and review. Market participants may upload trading materials to the trading system as electronic attachments; acceptance, review and information publication are completed online end to end."

## Notes

- Measure 7 promises 24-hour availability of the trading system ("确保市场主体可24小时通过线上完成相关交易").
- Whether the TECHDEAL app still exists after the 2025 portal redesign was not checked.
