# Macao government news on STEX's cross-border centre (July 2026)

## What it is

A Macao government press release on an agreement between Macao's trade-promotion institute (IPIM) and STEX's cross-border subsidiary (STEX-IC), signed in Lingang, Shanghai. **No local copy was saved.**

## What the docs take from it

- STEX's cross-border technology trade centre in Lingang, with **19 overseas nodes** (09 §2.2).

## Excerpts

None stored (no saved copy). Per the summary, the release describes STEX-IC as holding "an independent national technology trading licence", with 11 parks and incubators, 23 municipal technology-transfer agencies in 16 provinces, 19 overseas nodes and cumulative trade of RMB 113 billion; offshore settlement is mentioned only for a planned Macao centre.
