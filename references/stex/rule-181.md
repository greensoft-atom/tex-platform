# STEX rule 181 — Competitive negotiation

## What it is

Rule for selecting a buyer by negotiation with a panel of seller representatives and experts.

## What the docs take from it

- Odd-sized panel, **at least one-third experts drawn at random**; negotiations within **20 WD**; panel report within **5 WD** by majority (09 §2.4).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 5 — panel
   > 谈判小组由转让方代表和专家组成，成员为单数，其中专家的人数不得少于成员总数的三分之一。

   *Translation:* "The negotiation panel consists of representatives of the transferor and experts, with an odd number of members, of whom experts must be at least one-third."

   > 有关专家从专家库中随机抽取。

   *Translation:* "The experts are drawn at random from the expert pool."

2. Negotiation period
   > 谈判一般应在20个工作日内完成。

   *Translation:* "Negotiations should generally be completed within 20 working days."

3. Art. 13 — panel report
   > 在合格意向受让方提交最终报价后5个工作日内，谈判小组应根据谈判情况及合格意向受让方最终报价情况进行集中评议并编写评审报告

   *Translation:* "Within 5 working days after the qualified intended transferees submit their final offers, the panel conducts a joint assessment based on the negotiations and the final offers and prepares an evaluation report."

## Notes

- The "majority" rule in 09 §2.4 comes from Art. 14: if panel members disagree with the report, the panel fixes the recommended transferee list "按照少数服从多数的原则" (by majority). For complex projects the transferor may also recommend experts, subject to STEX approval (same article as the random draw).
