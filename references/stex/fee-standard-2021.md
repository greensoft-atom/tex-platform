# STEX fee standard (trial, 2021)

## What it is

STEX's published fee schedule for technology transactions and services, posted on 1 January 2021 under the rule pages of the 2020–2024 portal and still linked from rule and news pages in October 2024 ("相关信息" lists on `articles/138`, `/197`). It is the "收费标准" that STEX's rules and listings refer to. **The schedule itself is an image** (`upload/d/20201230/…png`) that no archive holds and that could not be fetched from the live site, so the **rates are unknown**. The page text says only that the standard is a trial and that actual business practice prevails.

## What the docs take from it

For the fee questions in 09 §2.6 and D-01:

- A formal STEX fee schedule exists (trial, dated 2021-01-01) and was still published unchanged in October 2024; no later version was found.
- Its amounts are **unknown**: the only machine-readable text is the disclaimer below. The 5 % buyer fee in [stex/bid-listing-9348](bid-listing-9348.md) is one listing's term, not the schedule.
- Who pays, from STEX's rules and 2026 listings (not from this page): both parties pay STEX a service fee "按照上技所的收费标准" within 5 WD of signing in enterprise procurement (rule 177 Art. 42), equity contribution (rule 195 Art. 37) and state-owned property-rights transfers (rule 807 Arts. 53, 65); 2026 technology listings make the transferee pay "按照上海技术交易所收费标准" and the transferor commit to pay "按照技交所收费办法" ([stex/bid-formal-listings-2026](bid-formal-listings-2026.md)).
