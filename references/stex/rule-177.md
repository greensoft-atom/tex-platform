# STEX rule 177 — Enterprise technology procurement

## What it is

Rule for companies that publish a technology need as a procurement listing and select a supplier through the exchange.

## What the docs take from it

- Publication **≥ 20 WD** (max one year); bid bond **≤ 2 %** of the budget; discriminatory conditions forbidden, e.g. naming a specific brand or patent (09 §2.4).
- Procurer and supplier (采购方 / 投标方) as user roles (09 §2.1).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 17 — publication period
   > 技术采购信息发布期限一般不少于20个工作日，最长不超过一年。

   *Translation:* "The publication period for technology procurement information is generally not less than 20 working days and not more than one year."

2. Art. 12 — bid bond cap
   > 投标保证金一般不超过采购项目预算金额的2%。

   *Translation:* "The bid bond generally does not exceed 2% of the procurement project's budget."

3. Art. 10 (4) — example of a discriminatory condition
   > （四）限定或指定特定的专利、商标、品牌或者供应商；

   *Translation:* "(4) restricting to or designating a specific patent, trademark, brand or supplier;"

## Notes

- Art. 10 lists five forms of unreasonable or discriminatory treatment of suppliers; Art. 9 lets the procurer set special supplier conditions only if they are not unreasonable. A supplier must have no major violations in the 3 years before taking part (Art. 25 (5): "参加企业采购活动前3年内，在经营活动中没有重大违法违规记录").
