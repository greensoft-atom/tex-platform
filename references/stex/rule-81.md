# STEX rule 81 — Publishing transaction information

## What it is

One of STEX's three 2020 operating rules for the listing pipeline (with [rule 82](rule-82.md) and [rule 83](rule-83.md)). It governs how STEX publishes a listing (技术交易公告): channels, minimum period, calendar and required content. The official site blocks automated access from outside China, so the text was read from an Internet Archive copy.

## What the docs take from it

- Disclosure lasts **at least 5 working days** "in principle", counted on the official working-day calendar (09 §2.3, diagram node "Public disclosure ≥ 5 WD"; 09 Summary item 3).
- Required announcement content: basic facts of the target, deal terms, buyer qualifications, material information, competitive method, deposit settings, start and end dates (09 §2.3 "Disclosure content").
- Requirement for a working-day calendar that follows official holiday notices (09 Summary item 3, 09 §6 "Time limits").

## Excerpts

1. Art. 4 — minimum period
   > 信息发布期限按照科技成果转化及国有资产管理相关政策法规要求，原则上一般不少于5工作日。

   *Translation:* "In line with policies and regulations on the transformation of scientific and technological achievements and on state-owned asset management, the information-release period is in principle generally not less than 5 working days."

2. Art. 5 — working-day calendar
   > 信息发布期按工作日计算，遇法定节假日以政府相关部门公告的实际工作日为准。

   *Translation:* "The release period is counted in working days; where statutory holidays occur, the actual working days announced by the relevant government departments apply."

3. Art. 6 — content of the announcement
   > 上海技术交易所发布交易信息内容应当与《技术交易公告》的内容一致，包括交易标的基本情况、交易条件、受让方资格条件、对技术交易有重大影响的相关信息、竞价方式的选择、交易保证金的设置等内容，并注明征集意向受让方的起止时间。

   *Translation:* "The transaction information STEX publishes shall match the Technology Transaction Announcement, including the basic facts of the target, the transaction conditions, the transferee qualification conditions, information with a material effect on the transaction, the choice of competitive method and the deposit settings, and shall state the start and end dates for soliciting intended transferees."

## Notes

- The doc paraphrases "原则上一般不少于5工作日" as "minimum 5 WD disclosure"; the rule allows adjustment on application ("如有特殊情况需要调整发布期限的，可以向上海技术交易所提交申请"), so 5 WD is a default floor, not an absolute one.
- The rule is marked 暂行 (interim) and dated 2020; whether it survived STEX's 2025 site redesign is unknown.
