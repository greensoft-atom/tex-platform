# STEX rule 195 — Technology contributed as equity

## What it is

STEX's rule for deals in which a technology holder contributes technology to an existing or new company in exchange for shares, with an investor or financing partner chosen through the exchange.

## What the docs take from it

- That STEX has a dedicated equity-contribution product with its own rules (09 §2 rule table; 09 §2.2).

## Excerpts

1. Arts. 2–3 — scope
   > 本细则适用于在上技所进行的技术作价投资折算股份或者出资比例的交易活动。

   *Translation:* "These rules apply to transactions on STEX in which technology is valued and converted into shares or a capital-contribution ratio."

   > 技术作价投资入股包括持有方将持有技术作价投资入股到已有公司或持有方与投资方新设公司,采用技术作价出资入股的方式。

   *Translation:* "Technology-for-equity investment covers a holder contributing its technology to an existing company, or a holder and an investor setting up a new company with the technology contributed as capital."

## Notes

- The rule uses its own role names: 持有方 (holder), 合作方 (cooperation partner: investor or financing party), 意向方 (intended partner) (Art. 4), which 09 §2.1 does not list separately.
