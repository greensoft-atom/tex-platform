# STEX example listing ZZC20260001 (id 9348)

## What it is

A real STEX listing (a lot of scrapped equipment, not a technology) with full bidding terms. Doc 09 uses it as a concrete example of how STEX parameterises online bidding.

## What the docs take from it

- Deposit **¥2,000**, paid from a company account; timed phase then **5-minute** continuous cycles; increments in ¥100 multiples, **max ¥1,000** per raise; no raising against your own top bid; deposit forfeited for fault in contracting (09 §2.4).

## Excerpts

1. Deposit
   > 1.竞价保证金：  2,000.00   元。

   *Translation:* "1. Bidding deposit: RMB 2,000.00."

   > 3.竞价保证金以竞价企业账户转账为准，不接受个人账户。

   *Translation:* "3. The deposit must be transferred from the bidding company's account; personal accounts are not accepted."

2. Continuous cycles and increments
   > 连续报价期由多个限时竞价周期组成，每个限时竞价周期为5分钟

   *Translation:* "The continuous period consists of several timed cycles, each of 5 minutes."

   > 最低加价幅度为人民币 100.00元，最高加价幅度为人民币 1,000.00元。

   *Translation:* "The minimum increment is RMB 100.00 and the maximum increment RMB 1,000.00."

3. No raising against oneself
   > 若某报价人为当前最高有效报价的报价人，则该报价人必须等到其他报价人报出新的有效报价后，方可在其他报价人报出的最高有效报价基础上继续加价。

   *Translation:* "If a bidder holds the current highest valid bid, it must wait until another bidder makes a new valid bid before raising again on that bid."

4. Fault in contracting
   > 将承担缔约过失责任

   *Translation:* "…shall bear liability for fault in contracting" (e.g. withdrawing a bid or not signing within 5 working days).

## Notes

- The page also states that a single bidder makes a one-shot bid ("当仅有一家竞买人参与竞价时…一次性输入有效报价").
- The page shows STEX's deposit settlement bank account numbers; they are not reproduced here.
