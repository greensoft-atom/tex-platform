# STEX rule 179 — Online bidding

## What it is

The rule for STEX's online bidding system: multiple-round ascending bidding (timed and continuous phases) and one-shot sealed bidding, pre-emption rights, confidentiality and contract timing.

## What the docs take from it

- Seller files a bidding plan; valid bidders get a **password slip** (竞价密码单) (09 §2.4).
- **Multiple-round mode:** first bid ≥ floor; raises in integer multiples of the increment; a **timed phase ≥ 10 minutes**; a **continuous phase of rolling cycles ≥ 60 seconds** where a new bid restarts the cycle and a cycle without bids ends bidding (09 §2.4).
- **One-shot mode:** highest wins; a tie goes to the earlier bid (09 §2.4).
- A statutory pre-emption holder makes one final bid at the top price; bidder identities and counts stay confidential; contract within 5 WD (09 §2.4).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 15 — minimum durations
   > 转让方应当根据竞价标的的具体情况在《竞价方案》中确定应价时间，其中定时报价期一般不少于10分钟，连续报价期内每一应价周期时间不少于60秒钟。

   *Translation:* "The transferor sets the bidding times in the Bidding Plan according to the target; the timed-bidding period is generally not less than 10 minutes, and each response cycle in the continuous-bidding period is not less than 60 seconds."

2. Continuous phase mechanics (Art. 14 explanatory paragraph)
   > 连续报价阶段有多个限时竞价周期，在每个周期内，如出现新的有效报价，则进入新的限时报价周期；在一个限时报价周期内如未出现新的有效报价，则当前有效报价方成为该次在线竞价活动的最高报价方，竞价终结。

   *Translation:* "The continuous phase consists of several time-limited cycles; if a new valid bid appears within a cycle, a new cycle starts; if no new valid bid appears within a cycle, the current valid bidder becomes the highest bidder and bidding ends."

3. Art. 13 — increments
   > 多次报价采用加价的方式进行，首次报价不得低于挂牌底价；竞买人交替报价，每次有效报价不低于该次竞价活动设定的加价幅度的整数倍，随即成为新的当前报价。

   *Translation:* "Multiple-round bidding is ascending; the first bid may not be below the listing floor; bidders bid alternately, and each valid bid, which must be at least an integer multiple of the set increment, immediately becomes the new current bid."

4. Art. 17 — one-shot tie-break
   > 竞买人的报价不得低于挂牌价，在有效的报价中，报价最高的竞买人为受让方；价格相同的，报价时间或提交报价单时间在先的竞买人为受让方。

   *Translation:* "Bids may not be below the listing price; among valid bids the highest bidder becomes the transferee; where prices are equal, the bidder whose bid or bid form was submitted first becomes the transferee."

5. Art. 19 — confidentiality
   > 转让方及其交易服务机构应当对意向受让方的名称、数量以及可能影响公平竞争的其他情况承担保密义务。

   *Translation:* "The transferor and its service institution owe a duty of confidentiality regarding the names and number of intended transferees and other circumstances that could affect fair competition."

## Notes

- Pre-emption: Arts. 5 and 11 ("…产生最高报价的竞买人在当前最高报价的基础上再进行一次报价，产生最终市场价格，该价格即为行权价格"): the highest bidder bids once more on top of the current highest price, and that final price is the exercise price. The current 09 §2.4 states it this way.
- Contract within 5 WD of fixing the transferee appears in the raw text line "上海技术交易所在确定受让方后5个工作日内，组织交易双方签订《技术交易合同》。"
