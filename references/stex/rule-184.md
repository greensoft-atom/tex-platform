# STEX rule 184 — Publishing supply and demand information

## What it is

Rule for listing technology supply or demand on STEX through a service institution: materials, completeness check, compliance review, period, withdrawal and exclusivity.

## What the docs take from it

- Materials: application, agency contract, identity, decision document, ownership proof (09 §2.3 diagram node A).
- Completeness check → receipt slip; compliance review **≤ 3 WD** (09 §2.3 node B).
- Period **≥ 5 WD, ≤ 1 year** (09 §2.3 node C).
- The agent verifies the client's materials and bears **joint liability** (09 §2.1).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 3 — agent liability
   > 委托交易服务机构进行交易的，受托机构应对委托方提交材料的真实性、完整性、准确性和有效性进行核实，并承担连带责任。

   *Translation:* "Where a service institution is appointed for the transaction, it shall verify the authenticity, completeness, accuracy and validity of the materials submitted by its client and bear joint and several liability."

2. Art. 6 — review time
   > 上海技术交易所应当在接收技术供需信息发布申请材料之日起3个工作日内，对《技术供需信息发布申请书》及附件材料的合规性进行审核。

   *Translation:* "STEX shall review the compliance of the application form and attachments within 3 working days of receiving them."

3. Art. 7 — period
   > 技术供需信息发布期限原则上不少于5个工作日，最长不超过一年，信息发布期限以在上海技术交易所网站发布的当日为起始日。

   *Translation:* "The publication period is in principle not less than 5 working days and not more than one year, starting on the day of publication on the STEX website."

4. Art. 12 — exclusive channel
   > 申请人以上海技术交易所作为唯一渠道进行技术供需信息发布的，不得进行场外交易。

   *Translation:* "An applicant that uses STEX as its exclusive channel for publishing may not trade off-exchange."

## Notes

- 09 §2.3 node C also says "changes restart the clock"; this is in the rule's amendment article (Art. 8 in the raw text), which the doc paraphrases.
- Art. 9: withdrawal (撤牌) decided within 3 WD of the application.
