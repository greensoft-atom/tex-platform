# Shanghai government news on STEX (14 January 2024)

## What it is

A news report on the Shanghai government portal about STEX's services, built around a ¥600 million patent transfer by Shanghai General Hospital (上海市第一人民医院). **No local copy was saved.**

## What the docs take from it

- 技知融: IP-pledge loan product built with banks; served **> 600 firms**; **¥3.285 billion** cumulative credit (09 §2.2).
- STEX's target of promoting 1,000 open licences by 2025 (research notes behind 09 §2.2 "Open licensing").

## Excerpts

None stored (no saved copy). Per the WebFetch summary, the page states 2023 transactions of 189.38亿元 with 4,336 results, the 技知融 figures (600+ firms, 403 pre-assessment reports, 61 formal reports, 32.85亿元 credit) and cumulative value "超325亿元" since the 2020 opening.

## Notes

- This article puts STEX's **2023** value at ¥18.938 billion; the fund prospectus gives ¥18.922 billion for 2023 (see [finance note](../finance/cmf-reit-prospectus-2025.md)). The small difference reflects different definitions or cut-off dates.
- The summary also reports 252 firms given credit totalling 37.22亿元 since opening, a different measure from the prospectus's "技术授信 40.58 亿元" (to July 2024).
