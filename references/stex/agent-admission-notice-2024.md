# STEX 2024 admission notice for practising agents

## What it is

A short STEX notice publishing, for objections, three firms admitted in 2024 as **执业机构** (practising service institutions, i.e. agents under rule 105). Its value for the docs is the list of legal bases it cites: two of the five STEX rules it names — a **supplementary rule on service-institution management** and an **interim rule on 科商 management** — and STEX's master **交易暂行规则** do not appear in the archived public rule list that docs 08–10 rely on ([stex/rule-105](rule-105.md) and the rule list in `policies_rules*`).

## What the docs take from it

Used as follows:

- In May 2024 STEX applied, besides rule 105, the rules 《上海技术交易所交易暂行规则》, 《上海技术交易所交易服务机构管理补充规则》 and 《上海技术交易所科商管理暂行规则》. None of these texts was found (not in the 2024 rule list, not archived, no search hit). The agent requirements in 09 §2.1 (from rule 105 alone) may therefore be **incomplete**: the supplementary rule could add or change conditions, fees or practitioner requirements.
- Admission runs as an annual intake ("2024年度") with a public notice and an objection period (published 14 May, objections until 23 May 2024, i.e. 7 working days after publication).
- The notice names the categories "执业机构" and "科商"; the 2024 portal lists these under 机构 → 执业机构名录 and 科商.

## Excerpts

1. Legal bases cited
   > 根据《上海市交易场所管理暂行办法》《上海技术交易所交易暂行规则》《上海技术交易所交易服务机构管理暂行规则》《上海技术交易所交易服务机构管理补充规则》《上海技术交易所科商管理暂行规则》经审查以下机构达到所申请资质的要求，公示如下：

   *Translation:* "Under the Shanghai Interim Measures for the Administration of Trading Venues, the STEX Interim Trading Rules, the STEX Interim Rules on the Management of Transaction Service Institutions, the STEX Supplementary Rules on the Management of Transaction Service Institutions and the STEX Interim Rules on the Management of 科商, the following institutions were found after review to meet the requirements of the qualification applied for, and are publicly announced as follows:"

2. Objection period
   > 截止日期至2024年5月23日，任何单位和个人对公示单位持有异议，均可在公示期间内反映。

   *Translation:* "Until 23 May 2024, any organisation or individual with an objection to the announced institutions may raise it during the notice period."

## Notes

- The three admitted firms are a media company, a health-technology company and an exhibition company, all with the category "执业机构"; the notice gives no criteria beyond "达到所申请资质的要求".
- The "《上海市交易场所管理暂行办法》" cited in May 2024 predates the 2025 measures of the same name ([policy/sh-trading-venue-measures-2025](../policy/sh-trading-venue-measures-2025.md)).
- **Privacy:** the original page shows a STEX staff contact with a mobile number, and the sidebar repeats two agent directory entries with contact persons' mobile numbers (the same entries appear unredacted in the earlier raw files `articles_159`, `articles_167` and `organizations_agency`); these were replaced with "[姓名已删除]" / "[电话已删除]" here.
