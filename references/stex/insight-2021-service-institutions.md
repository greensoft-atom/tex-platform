# STEX research note on Shanghai service institutions (2021)

## What it is

A policy analysis by STEX's own research institute, a year after STEX reopened, on the market-based technology-transfer service institutions that act as agents in Shanghai. It is the only STEX-published text found that discusses **how agents and practitioners are recognised**, and it says that no recognition system existed in 2021.

## What the docs take from it

For the practitioner question behind rule 105 ("2 or more practitioners recognised by STEX"):

- In 2021 STEX itself described the absence of a capability registration and recognition system for service institutions and of industry standards for service quality; it **proposed** one, together with a fee-guidance standard derived from it, and a professional-title route for practitioners based on the MOST-recognised junior/intermediate/senior technology-transfer certification (the Torch Center training).
- No later STEX document defining which practitioner qualifications it "recognises" was found. The likely evidence an applicant would show is the Torch Center 国家技术转移专业人员能力等级 training certificate (初级/中级/高级技术经纪人) or a 技术经纪 professional title, but this is **an inference, not a STEX rule**.
- Agent fees to clients were unregulated: the note proposes guidance "so that charging matchmaking service fees has a basis", i.e. agents set their own fees in the agency contract.

## Excerpts

1. No recognition system
   > （三）缺失技术转移服务机构能力登记和认定体系

   *Translation:* "(3) There is no system for registering and recognising the capabilities of technology-transfer service institutions."

2. Practitioner recognition proposal
   > 建议增设技术转移专业职称评定。可参考《北京市工程技术系列(技术经纪)专业技术资格评价试行办法》，以科技部认可的初、中、高级技术转移人才认证体系，结合实践案例，根据申报人的技术转移服务年限、服务案例、交易价值、课题研究等行业经历，探索与职称挂钩的评选方式。

   *Translation:* "We suggest adding a professional-title assessment for technology transfer. With reference to Beijing's trial measures for the engineering (technology brokerage) professional title, and based on the MOST-recognised junior, intermediate and senior technology-transfer talent certification system combined with practical cases, explore a selection method linked to professional titles according to the applicant's years of service, cases, transaction value and research."

3. Fee guidance
   > 由技术转移服务机构能力认定体系可衍生出相应服务费收取标准。

   *Translation:* "A corresponding standard for charging service fees can be derived from the capability-recognition system for technology-transfer service institutions."

## Notes

- Source line on the page: "来源：上海技术交易所创新研究院". It is an opinion piece, not a rule.
- The note cites 2019 figures (138 market-based service institutions in Shanghai, 6,276 staff) from "unpublished statistics".
