# STEX rule 83 — Organising the transaction and signing

## What it is

The third pipeline rule: what happens when the disclosure period ends with one or several qualified buyers, the contract-signing deadline, the minimum contract clauses and STEX's contract review.

## What the docs take from it

- One qualified buyer → deal at no less than the listing floor; two or more → the pre-announced competitive method (online bidding, auction, tender, competitive negotiation) (09 §2.3 diagram nodes H–K).
- Contract **within 5 WD** of the buyer being fixed (09 §2.3 node L).
- **Twelve mandatory contract clauses** plus patent particulars (09 §2.3 "Twelve mandatory contract clauses").

## Excerpts

1. Art. 2 — single buyer
   > 技术交易信息发布期满后，产生一个符合条件的意向受让方的，成交价格不得低于《技术交易公告》中的挂牌底价。

   *Translation:* "If one qualified intended transferee emerges after the release period ends, the transaction price shall not be lower than the listing floor price in the Technology Transaction Announcement."

2. Art. 6 — signing deadline
   > 上海技术交易所应当在确定受让方后5个工作日内，组织交易双方签订交易合同。

   *Translation:* "STEX shall organise the two parties to sign the transaction contract within 5 working days after the transferee is determined."

3. Art. 7 — patent particulars
   > 技术交易合同涉及专利的，应当注明发明创造的名称、专利申请人和专利权人、申请日期、申请号、专利号以及专利权的有效期限。

   *Translation:* "Where the technology transaction contract involves a patent, it shall state the name of the invention, the patent applicant and patentee, the filing date, the application number, the patent number and the term of validity of the patent right."

## Notes

- The twelve clauses in Art. 7 are: 项目名称; 标的的内容、范围和要求; 技术交易双方的名称与住所; 技术交易的方式; 交易价格、付款方式及付款期限; 合同的生效条件; 标的交接事项; 技术情报和资料的保密; 合同争议的解决方式; 合同各方的违约责任; 合同变更和解除的条件; 交易各方约定的其他事项. Art. 7 says "包括但不限于" (including but not limited to), so twelve is a minimum.
- Art. 9 (not used by the docs): STEX issues supporting documents when a contract needs government approval (antitrust, technology import/export).
