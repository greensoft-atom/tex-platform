# STEX rule 82 — Registering intended transferees

## What it is

STEX's operating rule for the buyer side of a listing: how an intended transferee (意向受让方) registers through a service institution, what it submits, how STEX reviews it and how the seller confirms qualification.

## What the docs take from it

- Intended buyers submit **through an appointed service institution (agent)**, which verifies the materials, issues a verification opinion and bears **joint liability** (09 §2.1 role table; 08 §1.1 "STEX rules require buyers to act through a registered agent, who verifies materials with joint liability").
- Materials: intent form with a **buyer floor price not lower than the listing floor**, agency contract, identity documents, decision documents, **risk statement** (09 §2.3 diagram; 09 §6).
- Review within **3 WD**; corrections within 3 WD; qualification letter to the seller **within 3 WD after the period ends**; seller replies within 3 WD and **silence counts as consent**; qualification notice states deposit amount and deadline (09 §2.3 diagram nodes E–G).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 4 — submission through an agent
   > 意向受让方应在信息发布期内，委托交易服务机构向上海技术交易所提交受让申请，并递交《意向受让申请书》及主体资格证明等相关附件材料，确认已知晓《技术交易公告》载明的所有内容和交易条件，并承诺遵守市场规则。

   *Translation:* "Within the release period, the intended transferee shall, through an appointed transaction service institution, submit a transfer application to STEX together with the Intended Transfer Application Form and supporting documents such as proof of legal status, confirm that it knows all contents and conditions in the Technology Transaction Announcement, and undertake to comply with the market rules."

2. Art. 6 — buyer floor price
   > 意向受让方的受让底价不低于转让底价。

   *Translation:* "The intended transferee's floor price shall not be lower than the transferor's floor price."

3. Art. 7 — agent verification and joint liability
   > 受托交易服务机构应当对意向受让方提交材料的真实性、完整性、有效性、合规性进行核实，出具核实意见，并承担连带责任。

   *Translation:* "The appointed transaction service institution shall verify the authenticity, completeness, validity and compliance of the materials submitted by the intended transferee, issue a verification opinion, and bear joint and several liability."

4. Art. 11 — qualification feedback to the seller
   > 对于通过齐全性和合规性审核的意向受让方，上海技术交易所应当在信息披露期满后3个工作日内向转让方出具《受让资格反馈函》，书面告知对登记的意向受让方的资格确认意见。

   *Translation:* "For intended transferees that pass the completeness and compliance review, STEX shall, within 3 working days after the disclosure period expires, issue a Transferee Qualification Feedback Letter to the transferor, stating in writing its opinion on the qualification of the registered intended transferees."

## Notes

- Art. 5 lists the materials (意向受让书, 交易服务机构委托合同, 主体资格证明文件, 相关决策文件, 风险提示书); Art. 12 sets the seller's 3-WD reply and deemed consent ("转让方逾期未予回复的，视为同意"); Art. 14 sets the deposit notice; Art. 15 gives a 3-WD objection window and a 5-WD seller review. All are in the raw text.
- Art. 2 says the transferee "可以…自主选择服务机构" (may choose its own service institution); the mandatory element ("委托交易服务机构") is in Art. 4.
