# STEX portal: online bidding system page

## What it is

The 2024 portal page introducing STEX's online bidding trading system (竞价交易系统), the back-office application behind `bid.stte.com`. It lists the system's management roles and processes.

## What the docs take from it

- Ten back-office roles: administrator, intake, first review, second review, settlement, settlement review, media publisher, certificate issuer / reviewer / printer (09 §2.1).
- "21 processes" from pre-disclosure to archiving (09 §2.3, 09 §2.7).

## Excerpts

1. Roles
   > 系统拥有十种管理角色，分别是管理员用户、受理组用户、初审组用户、复审组用户、结算组用户、结算审核组用户、媒体发布人员、交易凭证开具员、交易凭证审核员、交易凭证打印员。

   *Translation:* "The system has ten management roles: administrator, intake group, first-review group, second-review group, settlement group, settlement-review group, media publisher, transaction-certificate issuer, certificate reviewer and certificate printer."

2. Processes (opening of the list)
   > 主要分为二十一个流程，分别是预披露流程、转让申请流程、媒体发布流程、意向受让方登记流程、举牌申请流程、保证金划入流程、撤牌流程

   *Translation:* "It is divided into twenty-one main processes: pre-disclosure, transfer application, media release, intended-transferee registration, bid application, deposit in, withdrawal…"

## Notes

- 09 Sources cites the 2024-06-24 snapshot; the local copy is the 2024-10-14 snapshot of the same page. The role and process lists quoted above are what the docs reproduce.

- The page announces 21 processes but names only 20 (the list ends with 成交结果媒体公示流程、归档流程). 09 §2.3 reproduces the same 20 names under the heading "21 processes", so the doc is faithful to the source, including its inconsistency.
