# STEX example licence listing 20240034 (id 5383)

## What it is

A real STEX intent disclosure (意向披露) for licensing a technical-service result, tagged to two special boards. Doc 09 uses it for the licence price structure and the "notice period = due-diligence period" convention.

## What the docs take from it

- Board tags (气象技术交易专板; 科普专板), holder fields such as economic type (09 §2.3).
- For licences the buyer must state **entry fee + royalty** and whether payments are **milestone-based** (09 §2.3).
- "公告期即尽调期" — the notice period is the due-diligence period (09 §2.3).

## Excerpts

1. Due diligence
   > 1、本项目公告期即为尽职调查期，意向被许可方在挂牌公告期间，有权利和义务自行对标的进行全面了解。

   *Translation:* "1. The notice period of this project is the due-diligence period; during the listing notice, intended licensees have the right and duty to make their own full inquiry into the target."

2. Licence price structure
   > 交易金额应包含入门费及提成，付款条件应明确是否为里程碑式付款，以及相关里程碑阶段。

   *Translation:* "The transaction amount shall include the entry fee and royalties; the payment terms shall state whether payment is milestone-based and the milestone stages."

## Notes

- The listing shows "价格面议(场外结算)" (price negotiable, settled off-exchange) and "信息发布公告期（工作日）" (notice period in working days) of 256. The target is a children's reading book on the 24 solar terms licensed as a technical service, which shows how broadly "technical service" listings are used.
