# Tsinghua PBCSF report on Chinese and US technology trading platforms (2022)

## What it is

A 2022 research report comparing Chinese technology exchanges (STEX, CTEX, 科易网 and others) with US platforms. It is a university research publication, so only short excerpts are kept here.

## What the docs take from it

- STEX's valuation chain: big-data models, an expert price inquiry, then the market price ("model evaluation + expert price inquiry + market price") (09 §2.2).
- STEX's transfer-notice service: the example of 11 CAS patents priced by agreement, after which STEX issued a 《公示无异议证明》 (no-objection certificate) (09 §2.2 "Transfer notice").
- Both parties act through service institutions registered with STEX (09 §2.1, supporting rules 82 and 184).
- 科易网's "科易宝" system: 11,450 contracts, more than ¥7.7 billion signed (09 §4).

## Excerpts

1. Valuation method
   > 通过模型评价、专家询价和市场确价流程给予被评估技术资产相对合理的市场价格

   *Translation:* "through a process of model evaluation, expert price inquiry and market price confirmation, gives the evaluated technology asset a relatively reasonable market price"

2. Transfer-notice case
   > 公示无异议后，上技所出具了《公示无异议证明》，作为本次转让行为的见证。

   *Translation:* "After the notice period passed without objection, STEX issued a Certificate of No Objection to the Notice as evidence of the transfer."

3. Agents on both sides
   > 双方均需与在上技所完成注册的交易服务机构形成代理关系

   *Translation:* "Both parties must establish an agency relationship with a transaction service institution registered with STEX"

4. STEX scale by Q1 2022
   > 自开市至 2022 年第一季度，上技所累计成交额已达 39.2

   *Translation:* "From its opening to Q1 2022, STEX's cumulative transaction value reached 3.92 [billion yuan]" (the line continues "亿元" on the next line of the PDF).

## Notes

- 科易宝 figures are on PDF lines "目前累计签约合同 11,450 份，累计" / "签约金额超过 77 亿元人民币". The report is from 2022, so all platform figures are 2021–2022 values.
