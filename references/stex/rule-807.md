# STEX rule 807 — Public transfer of state-owned enterprise property rights

## What it is

STEX's trial rules for transfers of state-owned enterprise property rights (企业国有产权) through the exchange, following state-asset regulations: pre-disclosure and formal disclosure, buyer conditions, floor prices tied to the approved valuation, deposits, settlement and the transaction certificate.

## What the docs take from it

All in 09 §2.4 ("State-owned enterprise property rights" row):

- Pre-disclosure **≥ 20 WD** when control of the company changes; formal disclosure **≥ 20 WD**.
- Buyer conditions discouraged; if set, filed with the state-assets regulator before disclosure.
- First floor price ≥ the approved or filed valuation; relisting at **≥ 90 %** of it without further approval.
- Deposit **≤ 30 %** of the floor; instalments with **≥ 30 %** down within 5 WD, rest secured and paid within one year; certificate **within 3 WD** after price and fees arrive.

## Excerpts

1. Disclosure periods
   > 因产权转让导致转让标的企业的实际控制权发生转移应当进行信息预披露的，预披露公告时间应当不少于20个工作日。

   *Translation:* "Where the transfer changes actual control of the target company and pre-disclosure is required, the pre-disclosure notice shall run for not less than 20 working days."

   > 正式披露公告时间应当不少于20个工作日。

   *Translation:* "The formal disclosure notice shall run for not less than 20 working days."

2. Buyer conditions
   > 产权转让原则上不能针对受让方设置资格条件，确需设置的，不得有明确指向性或违反公平竞争原则，所设资格条件相关内容在信息披露前应完成报同级国资监管机构备案手续。

   *Translation:* "In principle no qualification conditions may be set for transferees; where truly necessary, they must not target a specific party or breach fair competition, and must be filed with the state-assets regulator at the same level before disclosure."

3. Floor price and relisting
   > 企业国有产权转让项目首次正式信息披露的转让底价不得低于经核准或者备案的转让标的评估结果。

   *Translation:* "The floor price at the first formal disclosure may not be lower than the approved or filed valuation of the target."

   > 信息披露期满未征集到符合条件的意向受让方的，转让方可以在不低于评估结果90%的范围内设定新的转让底价再次进行公告。

   *Translation:* "If no qualified intended transferee is found when disclosure ends, the transferor may set a new floor price of not less than 90% of the valuation and announce again."

4. Deposit and instalments
   > 保证金的交纳数额一般不超过转让标的转让底价的30％。

   *Translation:* "The deposit generally does not exceed 30% of the floor price."

   > 首付金额应不低于总价款的30%，并应在《产权交易合同》生效之日起5个工作日内支付

   *Translation:* "The first instalment shall be not less than 30% of the total price and paid within 5 working days after the property-rights transaction contract takes effect"

5. Certificate
   > 交易双方支付交易服务费用后，上技所应当在3个工作日内出具产权交易凭证。

   *Translation:* "After both parties pay the transaction service fees, STEX shall issue the property-rights transaction certificate within 3 working days."
