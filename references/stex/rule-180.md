# STEX rule 180 — Tender

## What it is

Rule for selling a technology by tender when several qualified buyers compete, run with a tender agent under STEX supervision.

## What the docs take from it

- Deposit **≤ 30 %** of the listing price; **≥ 10 WD** to prepare bids; evaluation **≤ 3 WD** after opening; contract **≤ 3 WD** after the award (09 §2.4).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 11 — deposit cap
   > 约定的保证金一般不超过挂牌价的30%。

   *Translation:* "The agreed deposit generally does not exceed 30% of the listing price."

2. Art. 12 — bid preparation time
   > 自招标文件发出至投标文件送达截止日，一般不少于10个工作日。

   *Translation:* "From issue of the tender documents to the deadline for bids, there are generally not less than 10 working days."

3. Art. 31 — evaluation
   > 评标工作一般应在开标后3个工作日内完成。

   *Translation:* "Bid evaluation should generally be completed within 3 working days after bid opening."

4. Art. 36 — contract
   > 招标人与中标人应当自招标结果通知书发出之日起3个工作日内，按照招标文件的要求和投标文件的承诺订立《技术交易合同》。

   *Translation:* "The tenderer and the winning bidder shall conclude the Technology Transaction Contract within 3 working days of the tender-result notice, according to the tender documents and the bid."

## Notes

- Art. 35: losing bidders' deposits are returned in full within 3 working days after the tender closes; the winner's deposit can be converted into the price.
