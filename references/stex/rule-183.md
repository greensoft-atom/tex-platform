# STEX rule 183 — Transaction archives

## What it is

STEX's records-management rule for transaction dossiers: filing deadline, retention classes, paper/electronic "dual sets" and access control.

## What the docs take from it

- One dossier per deal, filed **within 3 months** of completion (09 §2.5).
- Retention: **permanent** (landmark deals), **20 years** (normal deals), **10 years** (qualification files), **5 years** (valuation reports) (09 §2.5).
- Paper and electronic copies kept in parallel; access needs authentication and permissions (09 §2.5).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 10 — filing deadline
   > 技术交易文件材料归档时间，以完成一宗技术交易后3个月为限。

   *Translation:* "Transaction documents shall be archived within 3 months after a transaction is completed."

2. Art. 12 — retention classes
   > 属于普通案例和一般技术交易活动形成的档案，保管期限定为20年；反映技术交易相关主体资质的档案，保管期限定为10年；技术转让方提供的纸质资产评估报告（除总结报告外）可由上海技术交易所作为资料保存，保管期限定为5年。

   *Translation:* "Records of ordinary cases and general transactions are kept for 20 years; records on the qualifications of transaction parties for 10 years; paper asset-valuation reports provided by the transferor (other than summary reports) may be kept by STEX as reference material for 5 years."

3. Art. 17 — dual sets
   > 加快实现技术交易纸质和电子档案“双套制”管理

   *Translation:* "accelerate 'dual-set' management of paper and electronic transaction archives"

## Notes

- Permanent retention applies to "重要交易档案" such as landmark cases and first-of-kind trading modes (same Art. 12). Art. 16: permanent records are transferred to the municipal archives after 20 years. Art. 18 requires access permissions ("设置档案利用权限").
