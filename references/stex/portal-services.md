# STEX portal: service pages

## What it is

Four archived pages of STEX's 2024 website describing its services for research institutions (`/services`), enterprises (`/services/business`), regional governments and parks (`/services/gov`) and IP forensic appraisal (`/services/right`).

## What the docs take from it

- Product list in 09 §2.2: **正式挂牌** (formal listing), **意向挂牌** (intent listing), **非公开协议成交** (private agreement), **科技成果转化公示** (transfer notice with a notice-result certificate), **技术权益登记** (rights registration for results and service capability), evaluation products (pre-filing patent assessment, patent grading, income-method valuation), IP forensic appraisal.
- 09 §2.7: STEX sells a white-label "技术交易信息化综合服务平台" with a data cockpit and data middle platform to regions, so it is both a partner and a competitor.

## Excerpts

1. `/services` — formal and intent listing
   > 在上技所公开平台发布技术成果和技术服务交易信息，公开征集意向受让方。适用于交易标的、交易方式和交易底价明确的项目。

   *Translation:* "Publishes transaction information on technology results and technical services on STEX's public platform and openly solicits intended transferees. For projects whose target, method and floor price are clear." (正式挂牌)

   > 适用于标的范围、交易价格或交易方式不明确的项目，提供信息发布、征集意向方、组织交易等服务。

   *Translation:* "For projects whose scope, price or method is not yet clear; provides publication, solicitation of interested parties and organisation of the transaction." (意向挂牌)

2. `/services` — private agreement and transfer notice
   > 适用于在进场前已经达成交易并签订合法文件的交易双方，提供交易鉴证、资金结算、出具交易凭证等交易服务。

   *Translation:* "For parties that reached a deal and signed lawful documents before entering the exchange; provides transaction certification, fund settlement and issue of the transaction certificate." (非公开协议成交)

   > 面向研发机构、高等院校、医疗卫生机构持有的通过协议定价的科技成果，提供价格公示服务，并出具公示结果证明，可作为科研事业单位履行公示义务的凭证。

   *Translation:* "For results held by R&D institutes, universities and medical institutions and priced by negotiation: a price-notice service and a certificate of the notice result, which public research institutions can use as proof that they met their notice obligation." (科技成果转化公示)

3. `/services/gov` — white-label platform for regions
   > 围绕技术交易领域，搭建一套完整的数字化服务平台，为客户提供可定制化的技术交易信息化一站式平台。

   *Translation:* "Builds a complete digital service platform for technology trading, giving clients a customisable one-stop technology-trading information platform."

## Notes

- The `/services/gov` text continues "包含数据驾驶舱、数据中台、各类业务中台…" (data cockpit, data middle platform, business middle platforms); the line is truncated in the archived markup, so the doc's "core trading system, SaaS" items could not be confirmed verbatim from this copy.
- `/services/right` lists trade-secret, invention/utility-model, design, copyright (source-code identity) and trademark appraisals for courts and arbitration bodies.
- `/services/business` lists enterprise membership services (交易、技转通、融资、研究、营销), 科创属性 (STAR-market) assessment, patent freedom-to-operate evaluation and market-method patent valuation.
