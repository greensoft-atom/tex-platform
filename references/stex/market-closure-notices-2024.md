# STEX holiday closure notices (2024)

## What it is

Two short notices, addressed to STEX's transaction service institutions (agents), announcing that the exchange is closed and trading suspended during the State Council public holidays. They show how STEX's working-day timers relate to the official calendar ([policy/gov-holidays-2026](../policy/gov-holidays-2026.md) gives the 2026 calendar).

## What the docs take from it

Used as follows:

- STEX closes ("休市", trading suspended) on exactly the State Council holiday days and reopens on the next working day; it bases the closure on the State Council General Office's holiday notice. This supports FR-ADM-09 using the official calendar for STEX WD counts (rule 81 Art. 5 says the same for disclosure periods).
- STEX addresses such operational notices to "各交易服务机构", i.e. agents are expected to follow STEX's portal announcements; no machine-readable calendar feed was found.

## Excerpts

1. Basis and addressees (Labour Day 2024)
   > 尊敬的各交易服务机构：

   *Translation:* "Dear transaction service institutions:"

   > 根据《国务院办公厅关于2024年部门节假日安排的通知》（国办发明电〔2023〕7号）精神，现就上海技术交易所2024年五一劳动节休市安排公告如下：

   *Translation:* "In line with the State Council General Office's notice on the 2024 holiday arrangements (Guo Ban Fa Ming Dian [2023] No. 7), STEX announces its market closure for Labour Day 2024 as follows:"

2. Closure (Labour Day and National Day 2024)
   > 一、 本交易所于2024年5月1日（周三）至5日（周日）休市，共5天，休市期间暂停交易。

   *Translation:* "1. The exchange is closed from 1 May (Wednesday) to 5 May (Sunday) 2024, 5 days in total; trading is suspended during the closure."

   > 一、 本交易所于2024年10月1日（周二）至7日（周一）休市，共7天，休市期间暂停交易。

   *Translation:* "1. The exchange is closed from 1 October (Tuesday) to 7 October (Monday) 2024, 7 days in total; trading is suspended during the closure."

## Notes

- Both notices cite the State Council notice as "2024年部门节假日安排" (sic; the official title is "部分节假日安排").
- The make-up working days of 2024 are not mentioned; whether STEX trades on 调休 Saturdays/Sundays is not stated. The rules count "实际工作日" per the government announcement (rule 81 Art. 5), which implies that make-up days count as working days.
