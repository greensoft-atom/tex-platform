# STEX rule 182 — Auction

## What it is

Rule for selling a technology by auction on STEX. The auction itself is conducted by a licensed auction company; STEX organises, supervises and settles.

## What the docs take from it

- **Three-party agreement** between seller, agent (service institution) and a licensed auction house, filed with STEX (09 §1, 09 §2.4).
- Notice in provincial-level economic or financial media and on STEX (09 §2.4).
- One bidder → no auction, deal per the notice; none → extend or terminate (09 §2.4).
- Losing deposits refunded **within 5 WD** (09 §2.4).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 6 — three-party commission
   > 转让方应当在上海技术交易所受理交易申请前，与拍卖机构、交易服务机构共同签订委托合同，并将委托合同提交上海技术交易所备案。

   *Translation:* "Before STEX accepts the transaction application, the transferor shall sign a commission contract jointly with the auction company and the transaction service institution, and file it with STEX."

2. Art. 14 — one or no bidder
   > 在信息发布期限内只征集到一个符合条件的竞买人的，不再举行拍卖，由上海技术交易所按照技术交易公告的约定组织实施交易。

   *Translation:* "If only one qualified bidder is found within the release period, no auction is held and STEX organises the transaction as provided in the announcement."

   > 在信息发布期限内未征集到符合条件的竞买人的，由上海技术交易所按照技术交易公告的约定延长信息发布期限或者终结技术交易。

   *Translation:* "If no qualified bidder is found, STEX extends the release period or terminates the transaction as provided in the announcement."

3. Art. 21 — settlement and refunds
   > 其他竞买人的交易保证金应当在拍卖结束后的5个工作日内原额原途径返还。

   *Translation:* "Other bidders' deposits shall be returned in full, by the original route, within 5 working days after the auction ends."

## Notes

- Art. 4 defines the auction company as an enterprise legal person "具有拍卖资格" (with auction qualification) recognised by STEX; the doc's reference to the Auction Law is the authors' inference about why.
- Art. 9 sets the publication channels ("指定的省级以上公开发行的经济或者金融类报刊以及上海技术交易所网站"); Art. 22 links the transaction certificate to contract review and settlement.
