# STEX rule 105 — Transaction service institutions

## What it is

STEX's rule for the agents and professional firms that may act on the exchange: the two classes of service institution, eligibility, admission steps, annual review, change reporting and sanctions. Doc 09 cites it through the archived rule list page (`/policies/rules`), whose pages 1, 3 and 4 were also saved (page 2 had no usable snapshot).

## What the docs take from it

- Two classes: **practising agents (执业类)** and **professional providers (专业类)** such as auction, tender, evaluation, audit, legal, financial, patent-agency and consulting firms (09 §2.1).
- Eligibility of a practising agent: independent legal entity; registered **≥ 2 years**; registered capital **≥ RMB 1 million**; **≥ 2 practitioners** recognised by STEX; fixed premises; no major violations or dishonesty in the last **24 months** (09 §2.1 table).
- Process: review within **7 WD** → cooperation agreement within **15 WD** → fee → certificate (09 §2.1).
- Annual review in **July**; two consecutive missed reviews = exit; changes reported within **10 WD** (09 §2.1).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 5 (2)–(3), (5) — eligibility of practising agents
   > 注册成立2年以上，注册资金不得低于人民币100万元；（三）具备从事技术及相关股权、物权等交易业务经验，有固定的经营场所和必要的设施，2名或以上经上海技术交易所认可的具备相关执业资质的从业人员；

   *Translation:* "Registered for more than 2 years, with registered capital of not less than RMB 1 million; (3) experience in transactions in technology and related equity and property rights, fixed business premises and the necessary facilities, and 2 or more practitioners with the relevant professional qualifications recognised by STEX;"

   > 最近二十四个月不存在重大违法违规及失信行为

   *Translation:* "no major violations of laws and regulations and no dishonest conduct in the last twenty-four months"

2. Arts. 8–9 — admission timeline
   > 上海技术交易所自收到交易服务机构资格申请之日起七个工作日内完成资格审核，并将审核结果书面通知申请人。

   *Translation:* "STEX completes the qualification review within seven working days of receiving the application and notifies the applicant of the result in writing."

   > 申请人在上海技术交易所发出交易服务机构资格批准通知书之日起十五个工作日内，与上海技术交易所签订《上海技术交易所交易服务机构合作协议》。

   *Translation:* "Within fifteen working days of STEX issuing the approval notice, the applicant signs the STEX Transaction Service Institution Cooperation Agreement with STEX."

3. Art. 13 — annual review
   > 上海技术交易所对交易服务机构实行年度评议制度，凡连续两年未参加年度评议的交易服务机构视为自动退出。

   *Translation:* "STEX applies an annual review system to service institutions; any institution that fails to take part in the annual review for two consecutive years is deemed to have withdrawn automatically."

   > 交易服务机构年度评议时间为每年7月进行。

   *Translation:* "The annual review of service institutions takes place in July each year."

4. Art. 21 — reporting changes
   > 交易服务机构发生以下情形的，应自发生之日起十个工作日内向上海技术交易所报备：

   *Translation:* "A service institution shall report to STEX within ten working days of any of the following events:" (changes of legal representative, address, registered capital, business scope, investors, or key business managers).

## Notes

- Art. 4 defines the two classes; Art. 6 sets the professional-provider conditions; Art. 7 bars applicants with certain records, including joint objection by three or more existing institutions (三家以上（含）…), a criterion the docs do not repeat.
