# 沪科提复〔2023〕49号 — STCSM reply on research–industry platforms (2023)

## What it is

STCSM's official reply to a Shanghai CPPCC member's proposal. It describes NETC as a municipal functional platform and reports InnoMatch results since its August 2022 launch. As an official document of a state organ, its full text is committed in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/stcsm-reply-2023-hukefu-49.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- NETC is a "fourth-party platform" offering technology trading, technology finance and incubation (09 §3.1).
- The "一平台一展一赛" model: InnoMatch platform + Global Technology Transfer Conference + innovation challenge (09 §3.1).
- 2,617 demands from 1,385 firms; 581 solved; match rate from under 20 % to 46 % (09 §3.1 table, row "Challenge 2023").
- National technology-transfer talent training base: 742 junior, mid-level and senior technology managers trained (research notes; 03 §4 context).

## Excerpts

1. NETC positioning
   > 东部中心作为市级科技成果转化功能型平台，致力于打通高校、科研机构、企业间科技成果转化通道的第四方平台，提供技术交易、科技金融、产业孵化全链条服务。

   *Translation:* "NETC, as a municipal functional platform for the transformation of S&T achievements, is a fourth-party platform dedicated to opening channels between universities, research institutes and enterprises, providing full-chain services in technology trading, technology finance and industrial incubation."

2. One platform, one expo, one challenge
   > 一平台（全球技术供需对接平台）、一展（全球技术转移大会）、一赛（创新挑战赛）组合发力

   *Translation:* "one platform (the global technology supply-demand matching platform), one expo (the Global Technology Transfer Conference) and one challenge (the innovation challenge) working together"

3. InnoMatch results since August 2022
   > 自 2022 年 8 月发布以来，征集国际国内 1385家创新企业 2617 项需求，促成需求解决 581 项，意向或正式交易 18.3 亿元，对接率从不足 20%提升至 46%。

   *Translation:* "Since its launch in August 2022, it has collected 2,617 demands from 1,385 domestic and international innovative firms, helped solve 581 demands, with ¥1.83 billion of intended or formal transactions, and raised the match rate from under 20% to 46%."

## Notes

- **Discrepancy:** 09 §3.1 labels these figures "Challenge 2023". The reply attributes them to the **InnoMatch platform since its August 2022 launch** ("推动InnoMatch 全球技术供需对接平台建设 … 自 2022 年 8 月发布以来"), not to a 2023 challenge.
- The reply dates NETC's co-founding by MOST and Shanghai to 2015 ("2015 年，科技部与上海市共建"); MOST's own news says approval in November 2014 and unveiling in April 2015 ([most-2015-netc-opening](most-2015-netc-opening.md)).
