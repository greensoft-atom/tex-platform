# 沪科合〔2024〕17号 — 2024 Yangtze River Delta innovation challenge

## What it is

Joint notice of the four provincial science departments organising the 2024 challenge. It is one of the "2024–2025 notices" behind the challenge flow in 09 §3.5. Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/yrd-challenge-2024-hukehe-17.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- Collection on the InnoMatch challenge area **or** provincial platforms, with data shared among the four regions; rewriting by experts, service institutions and technology managers; only valid demands published; proposals submitted online; offline events; year-end awards for best solution, institution and technology manager (09 §3.5 diagram nodes A–J).

## Excerpts

1. Rewriting demands
   > 赛事主办方将委托专家、服务机构、技术经理人共同进行分析拆解，优化需求表述。

   *Translation:* "The organisers will commission experts, service institutions and technology managers to analyse and break down demands and improve how they are expressed."

2. Publication of valid demands
   > 结合需求方要求，对有效需求予以发布。

   *Translation:* "Valid demands are published, taking the demander's requirements into account."

3. Year-end awards
   > 挑战赛将于12月底前组织年度挑战者大会，评选优胜解决方案、优秀服务机构、最佳技术经理人等奖项，推广优质解决方案。

   *Translation:* "Before the end of December, the challenge will hold an annual challengers' conference to award best solutions, outstanding service institutions and best technology managers, and promote high-quality solutions."
