# 沪科〔2024〕216号 — 2024 YRD joint research demand collection

## What it is

STCSM's 2024 notice for the same programme as [沪科合〔2025〕5号](yrd-joint-research-2025-5.md). Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/yrd-joint-research-2024-huke-216.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- Same eligibility thresholds as 2025 (revenue ≥ ¥200 M in 2023, R&D ≥ 5 %, bounty ≥ 30 % and ≥ ¥2 M) (09 §3.5).

## Excerpts

1. Bounty share
   > 企业用于对外揭榜资金投入不低于项目总投入 30%，且不低于200 万元。

   *Translation:* "The firm's funding for the external bounty is not less than 30% of total project investment and not less than ¥2 million."
