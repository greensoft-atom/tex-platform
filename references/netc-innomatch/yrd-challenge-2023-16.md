# 沪科合〔2023〕16号 — 2023 Yangtze River Delta innovation challenge

## What it is

Joint notice of the science departments of Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang and Anhui organising the 2023 challenge run on InnoMatch (`challenge.innomatch.net`). Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/yrd-challenge-2023-hukehe-16.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- Demands are posted on `challenge.innomatch.net` ("我要发需求"); solvers submit proposals ("投递方案") (09 §3.5).
- **Every demand must receive a response**; several solutions → roadshow; strong competition → live final (09 §3.5, 09 Summary item 4).
- All parties sign an **NDA and a disclaimer**; the demander may hide information when publishing; a complaints and disputes mechanism (09 §3.5 "Rules worth copying").

## Excerpts

1. Every demand answered
   > 做到每个需求都有回应并力争得以解决。

   *Translation:* "ensure that every demand receives a response and strive to have it solved."

2. Roadshow and live final
   > 一个需求对应多个解决方案的，由承办单位或专场承办机构会同需求提出方通过路演等形式，遴选并确定优胜解决方案。

   *Translation:* "Where one demand has several solutions, the organiser or track organiser works with the demander to select the winning solution through roadshows and similar formats."

   > 经充分对接，仍因激烈竞争使需求方难以自主选择最佳解决方案的，可通过现场赛形式对解决方案进行评估和评比。

   *Translation:* "If, after full matching, intense competition still makes it hard for the demander to choose the best solution, the solutions may be assessed and compared in a live final."

3. Confidentiality and anonymisation
   > 参与挑战赛的创新需求提供方、解决方案提供方和评审专家均需按要求签订《保密协议》和《免责声明》法律文本。

   *Translation:* "Demand providers, solution providers and reviewing experts taking part in the challenge must sign the Confidentiality Agreement and Disclaimer as required."

   > 公开发布需求时可根据需求发布方意愿隐去相关信息

   *Translation:* "When publishing a demand, relevant information may be hidden at the demander's wish"

4. Complaints
   > 建立投诉和纠纷处理机制

   *Translation:* "establish a complaints and dispute-handling mechanism"
