# 沪科合〔2025〕27号 — 2025 Yangtze River Delta innovation challenge

## What it is

Joint notice for the 2025 challenge; it repeats the 2024 flow and adds industry/region tracks run by firms and service institutions, and international roadshows. Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/yrd-challenge-2025-hukehe-27.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- Same flow as 2024 (09 §3.5): demand description → analysis and rewriting by service institutions, technology managers and experts → publication of valid demands → online proposals → year-end awards. Sub-tracks (专场赛) run by companies and institutions (09 §3.3 "challenge campaigns with sub-tracks").

## Excerpts

1. Sub-tracks
   > 本年度挑战赛将继续支持科技型企业、专业服务机构等举办行业、区域等专场赛

   *Translation:* "This year's challenge will continue to support technology companies and professional service institutions in running industry and regional tracks"

2. Proposal submission
   > 针对发布的需求在线填报解决方案。

   *Translation:* "…submit solutions online against the published demands."
