# 沪科合〔2025〕5号 — 2025 YRD joint research demand collection

## What it is

Joint notice of the four provincial science departments collecting enterprise demands for the YRD "joint research" programme, whose key tasks are published as open bounty lists (揭榜任务清单). Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/yrd-joint-research-2025-hukehe-5.txt). The 2024 edition ([沪科〔2024〕216号](yrd-joint-research-2024-216.md)) uses the same thresholds.

## What the docs take from it

- Demander eligibility: revenue **≥ ¥200 million**, R&D **≥ 5 %** of revenue; external bounty **≥ 30 %** of the project budget and **≥ ¥2 million** (09 §3.5).
- Expert evaluation before publication (09 §3.5).

## Excerpts

1. Demander thresholds
   > 2024 年或 2023 年主营业务收入不低于 2 亿元，研发投入占主营业务收入比不低于 5%，无不良信用记录。

   *Translation:* "Main-business revenue in 2024 or 2023 of not less than ¥200 million, R&D spending of not less than 5% of main-business revenue, and no bad credit record."

2. Bounty share
   > 企业用于对外揭榜资金投入不低于项目总投入 30%，且不低于 200 万元。

   *Translation:* "The firm's funding for the external bounty is not less than 30% of total project investment and not less than ¥2 million."
