# 沪科规〔2022〕11号 — Shanghai innovation-voucher measures

## What it is

The joint STCSM/Finance Bureau measures governing Shanghai's science and technology innovation vouchers (科技创新券). Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/voucher-measures-2022-hukegui-11.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- Vouchers cover **50 %** of the approved service amount; **≤ ¥300k** per firm per year (¥100k per team); the provider files contract, invoice, payment proof and results; payments are made **quarterly** (09 §3.6; 03 §4 voucher box).

## Excerpts

1. Annual cap
   > 每家企业每年使用额度不超过 30 万元，每个团队每年使用额度不超过 10 万元。

   *Translation:* "Each enterprise may use no more than ¥300,000 per year and each team no more than ¥100,000 per year."

2. 50 % coverage
   > 并按该核定金额的 50%给予创新券使用额度。

   *Translation:* "…and grants voucher credit equal to 50% of the approved amount."

3. Redemption and quarterly payment
   > 服务完成后，服务机构在创新券平台上提交合同、发票、到款凭证、服务结果等有关专业服务实际完成情况的材料，申请兑付。

   *Translation:* "After the service is completed, the provider submits the contract, invoice, proof of payment received, service results and other evidence of completion on the voucher platform and applies for redemption."

   > 创新券平台常年受理兑付申请，每季度集中拨付一次资金。

   *Translation:* "The platform accepts redemption applications all year and pays out once per quarter."

## Notes

- [沪科〔2026〕225号](voucher-poc-2026-225.md) cites a newer 《上海市科技创新券管理办法》（沪科规〔2025〕6号）, which appears to have replaced these measures; it was not retrieved, so the 50 % rule should be re-checked against it.
