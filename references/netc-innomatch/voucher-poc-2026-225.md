# 沪科〔2026〕225号 — Proof-of-concept services under innovation vouchers

## What it is

STCSM notice adding full-process and single-step proof-of-concept (概念验证) services to the voucher catalogue. Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/voucher-poc-2026-huke-225.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- Proof-of-concept vouchers live from **21 August 2026** (09 §3.6).

## Excerpts

1. Launch date
   > 创新券平台（https://kjcxq.stcsm.sh.gov.cn/）将于 2026 年 8月 21 日 9:00，正式开通概念验证服务事项相关功能，并试运行约 30 天。

   *Translation:* "The voucher platform (https://kjcxq.stcsm.sh.gov.cn/) will open the proof-of-concept service functions at 9:00 on 21 August 2026, with a trial run of about 30 days."

2. Legal basis
   > 根据《上海市科技创新券管理办法》（沪科规〔2025〕6 号）

   *Translation:* "In accordance with the Shanghai Innovation Voucher Management Measures (Hu Ke Gui [2025] No. 6)"

## Notes

- The notice launched a ~30-day **trial run**; "live since August 2026" in 09 §3.6 is accurate as to the start date.
