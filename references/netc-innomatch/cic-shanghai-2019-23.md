# 沪科合〔2019〕23号 — 4th China Innovation Challenge (Shanghai), 2019

## What it is

Joint notice of three Shanghai authorities organising the 2019 China Innovation Challenge (Shanghai), the forerunner of the Yangtze River Delta challenge run by NETC. It includes the national (MOST Torch) challenge procedure. The copy was downloaded from a university website; the content is an official notice. Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/cic-shanghai-2019-hukehe-23.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- Prize money: winning challenger **¥100k**; best service institution ¥20k; top-10 solutions and top-10 demands ¥10k each; **¥2,000** per viable lead found by a service institution (09 §3.5).
- Prizes are paid **in principle by the demander** and cannot be made a condition for exclusive cooperation (09 §3.5; 09 §4 "prizes paid by the demander").
- Demand mining with a survey form → **demand analysis report** → selection (初选/优选) → cooperation agreement → **live final only under strong competition**; NDA and disclaimer mandatory (09 §4 China Innovation Challenge row).

## Excerpts

1. Prize payment and exclusivity
   > 满足企业技术需求的挑战者获得的奖励资金原则上由需求方支付。奖金仅用作奖励挑战者，不作为技术转让、技术许可或其他独占性合作的强制条件。

   *Translation:* "Prize money for challengers who meet a company's technology demand is in principle paid by the demander. The prize is only a reward for the challenger and is not a mandatory condition for technology transfer, licensing or other exclusive cooperation."

2. Service-institution lead reward
   > 对服务机构（不限于本市）帮助企业寻找到潜在解决方案，且企业认为有一定可行性的，择优以每项 2000 元奖励服务机构

   *Translation:* "Service institutions (not limited to Shanghai) that help a company find a potential solution the company considers feasible are rewarded, on merit, with RMB 2,000 per item"

3. Demand analysis report and live final
   > 填写“需求分析报告”并就下一步解决问题的方法路径提出建议。

   *Translation:* "…complete a 'demand analysis report' and propose the approach for solving the problem."

   > 经充分对接，仍引发激烈竞争致使需求方难以自主选择最佳解决方案时，可以通过现场赛形式进一步对挑战者及解决方案进行评估和评比。

   *Translation:* "If, after full matching, intense competition makes it hard for the demander to choose the best solution, challengers and solutions may be further assessed in a live final."

## Notes

- If a demander does not want a service institution, NETC "统筹提供基础服务" (coordinates basic services); fees between demander and institution are negotiated freely ("服务方式与服务费用由企业和服务机构自行协商"). Platform tools (result search, shared rooms) are free during the challenge. An earlier 09 wording ("base services are free") was revised to "basic search and matching services are free during the challenge", which matches the notice.
