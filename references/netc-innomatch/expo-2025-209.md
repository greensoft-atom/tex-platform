# 沪科〔2025〕209号 — 2025 Global Technology Transfer Conference exhibits

## What it is

STCSM notice inviting exhibits for the 2025 InnoMatch EXPO (Global Technology Transfer Conference, part of the Pujiang Innovation Forum, opening 20 September 2025). Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/expo-2025-exhibits-huke-209.txt).

## What the docs take from it

- Booth fees waived for technology SMEs with prior-year revenue **≤ ¥200 million** (09 §3.6).
- The "conversion clinic" (转化门诊) is part of the exhibitor benefits package (09 §3.6).

## Excerpts

1. Booth-fee waiver
   > 大会对以下情况免除展位费： 1.上年度营业收入不超过 2 亿元的科技型中小企业；

   *Translation:* "The conference waives booth fees for: 1. technology-based SMEs with operating revenue of not more than ¥200 million in the previous year;"

2. Five-edition totals
   > 五届以来共释放 21000 余项技术需求、35000 余项全国科技创新人才岗位需求，达成意向合作金额近 180 亿。

   *Translation:* "Over five editions it has released more than 21,000 technology demands and more than 35,000 S&T talent job demands, with intended cooperation of nearly ¥18 billion."

- The "1 + 3 + 365" model in 09 §3.6 is stated in the [STCSM 2024 report](../policy/stcsm-2024-progress-report.md) (and in news reports); "> 10,000 demands" comes from [the Shanghai government English report](innomatch-2025-shanghai-gov-en.md); neither is in this notice.
