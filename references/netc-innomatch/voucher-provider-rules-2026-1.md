# 沪科规〔2026〕1号 — Innovation-voucher service-provider rules (2026)

## What it is

STCSM's normative document on how service providers join the Shanghai innovation-voucher platform, their duties, annual evaluation and sanctions. Full text in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/voucher-provider-rules-2026-hukegui-1.txt). A 2025 draft for comment is in `raw/netc-innomatch/voucher-provider-rules-draft/` (no separate note).

## What the docs take from it

- Context for 09 §3.6 innovation vouchers: providers join the voucher platform and fulfil voucher orders; the 50 % / ¥300k / quarterly payment figures come from the voucher measures ([沪科规〔2022〕11号](voucher-measures-2022-11.md)).
- Research notes (not stated in the docs): onboarding without application ("免申请"), 7-day public notice before admission, annual evaluation of providers with 2+ years on the platform.

## Excerpts

1. Onboarding without application
   > 服务机构入驻创新券平台，采取“免申请”自主评价的方式

   *Translation:* "Service providers join the innovation-voucher platform through a 'no-application' self-assessment."

   > （三）通过核验后，在创新券平台上公示 7 天。

   *Translation:* "(3) After verification, the provider is publicly notified on the voucher platform for 7 days."

2. Validity
   > 本细则自 2026 年 2 月 28 日起施行，有效期至 2030 年 8 月31 日。

   *Translation:* "These rules take effect on 28 February 2026 and remain valid until 31 August 2030."

## Notes

- The 50 % coverage, the ¥300k annual cap and quarterly payment in 09 §3.6 are set by the voucher **measures** ([voucher-measures-2022-11](voucher-measures-2022-11.md)), not by these provider rules; the current 09 Sources lists both documents under "voucher rules". A later 《上海市科技创新券管理办法》（沪科规〔2025〕6号）, referenced in [沪科〔2026〕225号](voucher-poc-2026-225.md), was not retrieved.
