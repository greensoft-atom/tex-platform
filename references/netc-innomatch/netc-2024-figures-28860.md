Self-reported InnoMatch figures for 2024 (01 §2.3; 02 §3; 04 §2): **6,468** corporate demands, **4,390** technology results, **3,015** solutions, **313** completed transactions, **¥778 million** completed value, **> ¥3.4 billion** intended signing value.
