# Jiangsu technology exchange: agent commission schedule (2018)

## What it is

A published commission schedule of Jiangsu's provincial technology exchange for the "technology managers" (技术经理人, individual brokers) and broker firms (事务所) under its management. It is the only venue-published **agent commission** schedule found; no equivalent was found for STEX or CTEX. It shows how a Chinese venue expects agents to be paid: **by the clients**, as a tiered commission on the registered contract value, under a three-party intermediary agreement — not by the venue.

## What the docs take from it

A benchmark for 08 §7.1 and the "how agents are paid" question:

| Contract value | Technology transfer | Technology development | Consulting / service |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lowest band | < ¥50k: ¥3,000 | < ¥100k: ¥3,000 | < ¥20k: ¥1,000 |
| Next bands | ¥50k–0.5 M 6 % · 0.5–1 M 5 % · 1–3 M 4 % · 3–5 M 3 % | ¥0.1–0.5 M 4 % · 0.5–1 M 3 % · 1–3 M 2 % · 3–5 M 1 % | ¥20–100k 6 % · 0.1–0.3 M 5 % · 0.3–0.5 M 4 % · 0.5–1 M 3 % |
| Above | > ¥5 M: negotiated | > ¥5 M: negotiated | > ¥1 M: negotiated |

- The broker firm may keep **12 %** of the commission invoiced for its brokers (Art. 8).
- Two brokers of the same firm split **60 % (demand-side broker) / 40 % (supply-side broker)** (Art. 9).
- The basis is the contract value registered in the provincial technology-contract registration system (Art. 10); the broker signs a service certificate and a three-party "技术转移中介服务协议" with buyer and seller, which names who pays the commission and how (one-off, instalments or royalty share) (Art. 11, annexes 2–3).

## Excerpts

1. Agents earn commissions from the deals they broker (Art. 7)
   > 第七条 从事技术中介、居间等业务的技术经理人促成

   *Translation:* "Art. 7. Technology managers engaged in technology intermediation and brokerage who bring about [a contract shall receive a commission; the schedule is in Annex 1]"

2. Firm fee and split (Arts. 8–9)
   > 提供票据等相关服务，建议收取实际票据支付金额的 12%作

   *Translation:* "[the broker firm] provides invoicing and related services and is advised to charge 12 % of the amount actually invoiced as [a service fee]"

   > 提供技术需方信息并促成交易的技术经理人术获得全部佣

   *Translation:* "the technology manager who supplied the demand-side information and brought about the deal obtains [60 % of the total commission]"

3. Commission table row (Annex 1)
   > 5-50 万        6%        10-50 万       4%       2 万-10 万     6%

   *Translation:* "¥50k–500k: 6 % (transfer) · ¥100k–500k: 4 % (development) · ¥20k–100k: 6 % (consulting/service)"
