# NCSTI guide to listing service inventions (2021)

## What it is

A step-by-step guide with a case handled through CTEX. **No local copy was saved.**

## What the docs take from it

- Process model for university results: application → acceptance notice and publication → buyer registration and eligibility confirmation → one buyer: agreement; two or more: online bidding, competitive negotiation or comprehensive evaluation → deposit → deal confirmation → payment-received notice → certificate after fees (research notes behind 09 §4 and 09 §2.3).
- Materials include a listing application, commitment letter, **risk disclosure (风险提示书)**, decision document and patent list (09 §2.3 context).
