# CTEX "链证中国" digital evidence platform (2025)

## What it is

A CTEX news item on the launch of its blockchain evidence-preservation platform at the 2025 Zhongguancun Forum (27 March 2025).

## What the docs take from it

- Blockchain evidence service **"链证中国"** on CTEX's own chain "中技链", using **national (国密) cryptography** and **National Time Service Center timestamps**, with an "evidence + trading" model (09 §4; 09 §6 "Evidence").

## Excerpts

1. Technology
   > “链证中国”基于中技所自主研发的区块链“中技链”，采用国密算法构建安全可信的技术底座，实现数据加密、存证与核验全流程自主可控。

   *Translation:* "'链证中国' is based on CTEX's self-developed blockchain '中技链' and uses national cryptographic algorithms as a secure, trusted technical base, keeping encryption, evidence storage and verification fully under its own control."

2. Timestamps
   > 通过分布式存储技术与国家授时中心权威时间戳认证，确保数据权属清晰、存证时间精准、证据链完整

   *Translation:* "Through distributed storage and authoritative timestamp certification from the National Time Service Center, it ensures clear data ownership, precise evidence times and a complete chain of evidence"

3. Evidence plus trading
   > 同步创新“存证+交易”双轮驱动模式，对接中技所技术交易与数字版权交易系统

   *Translation:* "…and introduces an 'evidence + trading' two-wheel model, connected to CTEX's technology trading and digital-copyright trading systems"
