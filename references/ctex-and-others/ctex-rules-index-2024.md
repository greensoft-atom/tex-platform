# CTEX rule list (2023 and 2024 captures)

## What it is

The "中技所交易规则" list on CTEX's website at two dates. Comparing them shows which CTEX rules changed in 2023–2024. Only the list was archived for most 2024 rules; their texts were not found.

## What the docs take from it

Used as follows:

- **Current in November 2024:** 技术交易规则 (2023-11-06, see [ctex-and-others/ctex-trading-rules-2023](ctex-trading-rules-2023.md)); a new **技术交易收费办法 (2024-06-26)**; new 细则 of 2024-06-26 for information publication, transfer, licensing, equity contribution, procurement, suspension/termination and investor suitability; **会员管理办法 (2023-12-19)**; dispute mediation (2023-11-06); deal-results publication guideline (2023-04-06).
- **Gone from the 2024 list:** the 服务机构管理办法 (listed 2021-04-29; its 2022 text is in [ctex-and-others/ctex-service-institution-measures-2022](ctex-service-institution-measures-2022.md)) and the 2021 fee measures — consistent with agents now being handled as **members** and with the 2021 fees having been replaced.
- Consequence for the docs: CTEX's fee amounts, agent (member) requirements and the 2024 publication 细则 are **unknown in their current form**; the 2021/2022 texts are the latest available.
