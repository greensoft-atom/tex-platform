# CTEX service-institution measures (2022)

## What it is

CTEX's rules for the organisations that may act as its service institutions, including **transaction service institutions** (agents that source, promote and support deals). It is the CTEX counterpart of STEX rule 105 ([stex/rule-105](../stex/rule-105.md)). The measures no longer appear on CTEX's rule list of November 2024, which instead lists a **会员管理办法 (membership measures) dated 2023-12-19**; the agent regime has probably moved to that membership system, whose text was not found ([ctex-and-others/ctex-member-centre](ctex-member-centre.md)).

## What the docs take from it

Requirements for a CTEX agent, as of 2022–2023:

- Four classes: transaction, IP, consulting and international-cooperation service institutions (Art. 5).
- Conditions (Art. 6): legally established legal person or other organisation; approved business scope and the relevant licences or qualifications; good reputation and **no violations in the last 3 years**; fixed premises; internal management and risk systems. **No** minimum registered capital, years in business, staff number or broker certificate (unlike STEX rule 105).
- Application file (Art. 7): application form, integrity agreement, cooperation agreement, business licence, qualification proof, premises proof for ≥ 1 year, company profile, legal representative's ID.
- CTEX reviews within **10 WD** (Art. 8); approved applicants sign the cooperation and integrity agreements and receive a certificate **valid one year** (Art. 9); annual inspection: notice by 15 October, inspections 1 November–20 December (Art. 12); changes reported within 10 WD (Art. 14).
- Conflict rule: in one deal, an institution may not act for both sides, nor for two or more intended buyers or investors (Art. 18), and may not take part in acquiring a project it is transferring, licensing or financing (Art. 19).
- Institutions owe CTEX fees ("服务机构应交的各项费用", Art. 22 (4)); no amount is given and nothing is said about commissions or fee sharing.

## Excerpts

1. Definition (Art. 5, 2nd para.)
   > 交易服务机构是指接受委托，为中技所承接的技术交易项目提供开发、推介、交易辅助等基础服务的法人或非法人组织。

   *Translation:* "Transaction service institutions are legal persons or other organisations that, on commission, provide basic services such as sourcing, promotion and transaction support for technology-transaction projects undertaken by CTEX."

2. Certificate valid one year (Art. 9)
   > 审核通过的，申请人与中技所签订《服务机构合作协议》、《服务机构廉洁从业协议书》，领取《证书》，获得中技所服务机构资格（有效期一年）。

   *Translation:* "If approved, the applicant signs the Service Institution Cooperation Agreement and the Integrity Agreement with CTEX, receives the certificate and obtains CTEX service-institution status (valid for one year)."

3. Conflict rule (Art. 18)
   > 第十八条 通过中技所公开交易的同一宗项目中，同一交易服务机构原则上不得同时接受交易双方的委托，也不得同时接受两个及以上意向受让方/投资方的委托。

   *Translation:* "Art. 18. In the same project publicly traded through CTEX, a transaction service institution shall in principle not accept mandates from both parties, nor from two or more intended transferees/investors."

4. Fees owed (Art. 22 (4))
   > （四）未及时足额交纳服务机构应交的各项费用，经中技所通知仍未交纳的；

   *Translation:* "(4) failing to pay in time and in full the fees a service institution owes, and still not paying after notice from CTEX;" (a ground for sanctions)

## Notes

- Art. 27: in force 2022-12-20; the 2021-04-27 version was repealed.
- The 2023 trading rules call agents "中技所交易服务商" and require an agency contract with the client (Art. 7 of [ctex-and-others/ctex-trading-rules-2023](ctex-trading-rules-2023.md)).
