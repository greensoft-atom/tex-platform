# CTEX technology transfer rules (trial, 2020)

## What it is

CTEX's implementing rules for technology-transfer listings, in force from August 2020. Doc 09 §4 uses their instalment and timing terms (see the attribution note in [ctex-and-others/ctex-trading-rules-2023](ctex-trading-rules-2023.md)). CTEX's rule list of November 2024 shows a replacement dated 2024-06-26 whose text was not found, so these terms may have changed ([ctex-and-others/ctex-rules-index-2024](ctex-rules-index-2024.md)).

## What the docs take from it

- Publication ≥ 10 WD from the day after publication; ≤ 12 months (Art. 8).
- Both sides pay CTEX's service fees within **3 WD** of signing (Art. 27).
- Instalments: down payment **≥ 30 %** within 5 WD of the contract taking effect; the rest secured by a guarantee acceptable to the transferor, with interest, within **1 year** (09 §4).
- Payout to the transferor within **5 WD** of the transfer request (Art. 32); certificate within **3 WD** of receiving price and fees (Art. 33) (09 §4).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 8 — publication period
   > 第八条 发布交易信息时间应当不少于10个工作日，自中技所官方网站发布次日为起计算。

   *Translation:* "Art. 8. Transaction information shall be published for not less than 10 working days, counted from the day after publication on CTEX's official website."

2. Art. 27 — both sides pay
   > 第二十七条 交易双方签订技术交易合同3个工作日内，转让方和受让方应当按照中技所的收费标准分别向中技所交纳服务费。

   *Translation:* "Art. 27. Within 3 working days of signing the technology transaction contract, the transferor and the transferee shall each pay CTEX service fees according to CTEX's fee standard."

3. Instalments
   > 交易价款金额较大、一次付清确有困难的，可以采取分期付款方式，但首付交易价款数额不低于总价款的30%，并在技术交易合同生效之日起5个工作日内支付，其余款项应当提供转让方认可的合法有效担保，并按同期银行贷款利率支付延期付款期间的利息，付款期限不得超过1年。

   *Translation:* "Where the price is large and payment in one sum is genuinely difficult, it may be paid in instalments, but the down payment shall be at least 30 % of the total and be paid within 5 working days of the contract taking effect; the remainder shall be secured by a lawful and valid guarantee accepted by the transferor, with interest at the bank lending rate for the deferral period, and the payment period may not exceed 1 year."

4. Art. 33 — certificate
   > 第三十三条 中技所应在收到交易价款及服务费后3个工作日内出具技术交易凭证。

   *Translation:* "Art. 33. CTEX shall issue the technology transaction certificate within 3 working days of receiving the transaction price and the service fees."
