# Tianjin technology trading platform fee schedule (2021)

## What it is

The fee schedule of Tianjin's technology trading platform, the only published venue fee schedule found. **No local copy was saved.**

## What the docs take from it

- Seller, tiered cumulative: **2 %** up to ¥1 M · **1 %** ¥1–10 M · **0.5 %** ¥10–100 M · **0.3 %** above ¥100 M; minimum ¥2,000. Buyer ≤ 5 %. Bidding fee ≥ 5 %, minimum ¥5,000 per side. Notice and value-added fees separate (09 §2.6).
