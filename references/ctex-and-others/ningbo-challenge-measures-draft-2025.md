# Ningbo innovation challenge measures (2025 draft for comment)

## What it is

A government draft published for public comment: the drafting explanation (Annex 1) and the revised draft measures of the Ningbo S&T Bureau for its innovation challenge. As a document of a state organ it is committed in [`official-texts/`](../official-texts/ningbo-challenge-measures-draft-2025.txt); it is a **draft**, not final law.

## What the docs take from it

- A live final needs **at least three** teams on a list (09 §4).
- Contract with the first-ranked team **within two months** of the live final (09 §4).
- Subsidy paid in three tranches: first ≤ **40 %**, cumulative ≤ **80 %** after mid-term review, rest after acceptance (09 §4).

## Excerpts

1. Minimum three teams
   > 一个榜单须至少三个技术团队入围现场赛，少于三个技术团队的榜单不开展现场挑战

   *Translation:* "A list must have at least three technical teams shortlisted for the live final; lists with fewer than three teams do not hold a live challenge"

2. Contract within two months
   > 现场赛后两个月内，“重大技术需求出题企业”与该需求现场挑战第一名的揭榜团队签订技术合同

   *Translation:* "Within two months after the live final, the company that posed the major technical demand signs a technology contract with the team ranked first in the live challenge"

3. Staged subsidy
   > 资助经费分3批拨付，首批拨付额度根据实际发生委托研发费用且不超过资助总额的40%拨付；项目中期检查通过后，第二批拨付额度根据已支付的委托研发费用且拨付额度累计不超过资助总额的80%拨付；剩余资金待全部委托研发费用支付完结且项目通过验收后拨付。

   *Translation:* "Funding is paid in three tranches: the first, based on commissioned R&D costs actually incurred, up to 40% of the total; after the mid-term review, the second, based on commissioned R&D costs paid, up to a cumulative 80%; the rest after all commissioned R&D costs are paid and the project passes acceptance."

## Notes

- 09 §4 groups this with Jiangsu under "Ningbo / Jiangsu challenges"; the three rules above are Ningbo's (draft), not Jiangsu's.
