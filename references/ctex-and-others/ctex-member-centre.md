# CTEX member platform

## What it is

CTEX's member platform ("中技所会员", branded 技E网), where its agents and other service firms are managed. The 2024 pages list the membership measures and forms and announce annual inspections; the 2017 home page shows the earlier member categories, including **经纪会员 (broker members)** and individually certified **技术经理人**.

## What the docs take from it

Used as follows:

- Since December 2023 CTEX runs its agents and service firms as **members**: two types, **交易会员 (trading members)** and **专业会员 (professional members)**, under the 会员管理办法 (2023-12-19; download dated 2024-01-17). Forms: cooperation agreement, integrity agreement, annual-inspection report, change form, deregistration form — i.e. the same admission/annual-inspection pattern as the 2022 service-institution measures. The text of the membership measures, and any membership fee, were **not found**.
- Annual inspection: 68 institutions passed the 2023 member inspection (news of 2024-01-17); earlier years speak of "服务机构年检".
- In 2017 CTEX had channel, capability and service members plus **technology managers** (individuals) with an annual certification form ("技术经理人年度认证申请表") and a broker-member application form — CTEX once recognised individual practitioners itself; whether this continues is unknown.
