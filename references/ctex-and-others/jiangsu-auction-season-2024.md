# Jiangsu 2024 auction season and J-TOP challenge season plans

## What it is

Three annexes of a Jiangsu programme notice, published by the provincial technology property exchange. The issuing body of the parent notice is not shown, so the file is treated as a non-official document (excerpts only).

## What the docs take from it

- J-TOP challenge stages: **寻榜 → 发榜 → 揭榜 → 竞榜 → 跟踪问效** (find → publish → respond → compete → track results) (09 §4).
- Online auctions every **Wednesday and Friday** on the provincial platform's "拍卖季" page (09 §4 "weekly online auction days").
