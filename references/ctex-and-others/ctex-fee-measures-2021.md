# CTEX fee measures (2021)

## What it is

CTEX's published fee schedule for technology transactions, in force from 1 June 2021 (replacing a version of 2020-08-19). It is the only complete venue fee schedule found for CTEX. The CTEX rule list of November 2024 shows a **newer 收费办法 dated 2024-06-26** whose text could not be retrieved, so the 2021 amounts may have changed ([ctex-and-others/ctex-rules-index-2024](ctex-rules-index-2024.md)). The earlier trial version (收费办法（试行）) is kept from a third-party reprint for comparison.

## What the docs take from it

CTEX fee benchmark for 09 §2.6:

| Fee | 2021 schedule | Who pays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listing (挂牌服务费) | ¥2,000 per item; waived for exclusive listings run fully at CTEX, or listings from a regional market with a joint-listing agreement; can be set off against the transaction fee | client (委托方) |
| Transaction, agreement deal (one buyer) | marginal tiers on the deal value: **1.5 %** ≤ ¥1 M · **0.5 %** ¥1–10 M · **0.25 %** ¥10–100 M · **0.15 %** > ¥100 M; min. ¥3,000 per side | **both sides**, each charged ("单笔单向") |
| Transaction, competitive deal (≥ 2 buyers) | holder: agreement tiers on the floor price + **5 %** on the premium above it; buyer: agreement tiers; min. ¥5,000 per side | both sides |
| Results publication (公示服务费) | ¥3,000 per item | client |
| Settlement (结算服务费) | marginal tiers **1 % / 0.4 % / 0.2 % / 0.1 %** on the same bands; min. ¥3,000 per side | client using CTEX settlement |
| Other services (consulting, roadshow, promotion, evaluation, legal-status search) | by separate contract | client |

- A third party may pay on the client's behalf; special cases are negotiated.
- Trial version (reprint posted 2019-03-31): agreement deals **3 % / 1 % / 0.5 % / 0.3 %**, min. ¥3,000; competitive deals **≥ 5 %** of the deal price, min. ¥5,000 per side; auctions and tenders negotiated.
- The 10 % buyer bidding fee in 09 §2.6 comes from one CTEX bidding notice and is a listing-specific term, above both schedules.

## Excerpts

1. Listing fee
   > 挂牌服务费指委托方委托中技所挂牌技术项目支付的费用，单笔单项收费为2,000元人民币。

   *Translation:* "The listing fee is the fee paid by a client who entrusts CTEX with listing a technology project; RMB 2,000 per item."

2. Transaction fee — both sides
   > 交易服务费指委托方在中技所挂牌技术项目，通过协议或竞价（包括网络竞价、竞争性谈判、综合评议）方式成交，双方支付的费用。

   *Translation:* "The transaction service fee is the fee paid by both parties when a project listed at CTEX is concluded by agreement or by competitive methods (network bidding, competitive negotiation, comprehensive evaluation)."

   > 差额计费率为单笔单向计费比率。单笔单向收费不低于3,000元人民币。

   *Translation:* "The marginal rate is charged per transaction per side. The fee per transaction per side is not less than RMB 3,000."

3. Competitive deals
   > 对于持有方支付的服务费，挂牌底价部分按照协议成交标准收取服务费，挂牌溢价部分按照5%费率收取服务费。

   *Translation:* "For the fee paid by the holder, the part up to the listing floor price is charged at the agreement-deal rates and the premium above the floor price at 5 %."

4. Trial version (third-party reprint)
   > 竞价服务费收费标准为不低于成交金额的5%，单笔单向竞价服务费不低于5,000元人民币;

   *Translation:* "The bidding service fee is not less than 5 % of the deal value, and not less than RMB 5,000 per transaction per side;"

## Notes

- The schedule's tiered table is in the raw text as separate lines (band, rate); the table above reproduces it.
- A second reprint of the 2021 text on a CTEX-built platform (gbatech.pkusp.com, posted 2021-09-22) was found and not kept (same content).
- Art. 30 of the 2023 trading rules ([ctex-and-others/ctex-trading-rules-2023](ctex-trading-rules-2023.md)) and Art. 27 of the 2020 transfer rules ([ctex-and-others/ctex-transfer-rules-2020](ctex-transfer-rules-2020.md)) make both parties pay CTEX per this schedule (2020: within 3 WD of signing).
- The schedule contains no fee for agents and no commission sharing with agents.
