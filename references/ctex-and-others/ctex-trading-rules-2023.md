# CTEX Technology Trading Rules (2023)

## What it is

CTEX's master trading rules. **No local copy (cited from web search)**: ctex.cn blocks automated user agents.

## What the docs take from it

- Disclosure **≥ 10 WD** from the day after publication; total **≤ 12 months**; deposit generally **≤ 30 %** of the floor price (09 §4).
- Methods: network bidding, dynamic quotation, auction, competitive negotiation, comprehensive evaluation (09 §4 context).
- From CTEX's **2020 transfer rules** (技术转让细则), not the 2023 rules: price within 5 WD of the contract taking effect; instalments with **≥ 30 % down**, the rest guaranteed and paid within 1 year (09 §4).
- From a single CTEX bidding notice (竞买须知): free-bid period then 5-minute timed cycles; buyer bidding service fee **10 %**; fees due within 3 WD of signing (09 §2.6 "CTEX (one auction listing)").
