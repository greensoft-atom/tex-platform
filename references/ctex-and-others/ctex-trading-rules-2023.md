# CTEX Technology Trading Rules (2023)

## What it is

CTEX's master trading rules, in force since 1 November 2023, replacing the 2020 trial rules. ctex.cn blocks automated and foreign requests, so the copy is the Wayback capture of the us.ctex.cn page (same path as the www.ctex.cn URL cited in the docs). On the CTEX rule list of 4 November 2024 these rules are still the current version ([ctex-and-others/ctex-rules-index-2024](ctex-rules-index-2024.md)).

## What the docs take from it

- Disclosure **≥ 10 WD** from the day after publication on CTEX's website; total **≤ 12 months**; each extension ≥ 10 WD (Art. 18); deposit generally **≤ 30 %** of the floor price for transfers (09 §4; FR-VEN-03 "≥ 10 WD on CTEX" is **confirmed**). The rule speaks of "发布交易信息时间" / "信息发布期", not "信息披露期".
- Methods: network bidding, dynamic quotation, auction, competitive negotiation, comprehensive evaluation (Art. 11) (09 §4 context).
- Holders and buyers may act through a **CTEX transaction service provider** under an agency contract (Art. 7); all parties pay CTEX's service fees after signing (Art. 30); certificate within 3 WD of receiving price and fees (Art. 33).
- From CTEX's **2020 transfer rules** (技术转让细则), not the 2023 rules: instalments with **≥ 30 % down** within 5 WD, the rest guaranteed and paid within 1 year; payout to the transferor within 5 WD ([ctex-and-others/ctex-transfer-rules-2020](ctex-transfer-rules-2020.md)) (09 §4).
- From a single CTEX bidding notice (竞买须知): free-bid period then 5-minute timed cycles; buyer bidding service fee **10 %**; fees due within 3 WD of signing (09 §2.6 "CTEX (one auction listing)"); compare the published fee schedule in [ctex-and-others/ctex-fee-measures-2021](ctex-fee-measures-2021.md).

## Excerpts

1. Art. 15 — disclosure period
   > 第十五条 发布交易信息时间不少于10个工作日，自中技所官方网站发布次日起计算。信息发布期自首次发布之日起累计不超过12个月。

   *Translation:* "Art. 15. Transaction information shall be published for not less than 10 working days, counted from the day after publication on CTEX's official website. The publication period may not exceed 12 months in total from the first publication."

2. Art. 7 — agents
   > 第七条 持有方和意向方可以委托中技所交易服务商进行技术交易。委托中技所交易服务商进行交易的，应当与中技所交易服务商签订委托合同。

   *Translation:* "Art. 7. Holders and intended counterparties may entrust a CTEX transaction service provider with the technology transaction. Those who do so shall sign an agency contract with the CTEX transaction service provider."

3. Art. 30 and Art. 33 — fees and certificate
   > 第三十条 技术交易合同签订后，交易各方应当按照中技所收费标准支付服务费。

   *Translation:* "Art. 30. After the technology transaction contract is signed, all parties shall pay service fees according to CTEX's fee standard."

   > 第三十三条 中技所在收到交易价款及服务费用后3个工作日内出具交易凭证。

   *Translation:* "Art. 33. CTEX issues the transaction certificate within 3 working days of receiving the transaction price and the service fees."

4. Deposit cap (Art. 12)
   > 对于技术转让的标的，设定的保证金一般不超过标的底价的30%。

   *Translation:* "For technology-transfer objects, the deposit set is generally not more than 30 % of the floor price."

## Notes

- **Attribution:** 09 §4 introduces the list with "Rules (2023):" but the instalment terms, the certificate-within-3-WD and payout-within-5-WD items come from CTEX's 2020 transfer rules; the 2023 rules repeat the 3-WD certificate (Art. 33) but leave instalments to the contract (Art. 31).
- Art. 46: in force 1 November 2023; the 2020 trial rules (in force 2020-08-19, same 10-WD rule in their Art. 15) were repealed. The 2020 trial copy found in the follow-up research was not kept (superseded).
- On 2024-06-26 CTEX issued new implementing rules, including a 发布交易信息操作细则 and a 技术转让细则; their texts were not found, so whether they change the period is **unknown** ([ctex-and-others/ctex-rules-index-2024](ctex-rules-index-2024.md)).
