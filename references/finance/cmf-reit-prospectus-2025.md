# Fund prospectus with STEX and NETC financials (2025 update)

## What it is

The updated prospectus (513 pages) of a public infrastructure REIT holding incubator parks of the Yangpu group. STEX and NETC's operating company are tenants ("important cash-flow providers"), so §14 discloses their business and financial data. It is a commercial securities document; only short excerpts are kept here.

## What the docs take from it

- STEX: founded 1993 by MOST and Shanghai; filing accepted 31 Dec 2019; re-opened 28 Oct 2020; four core functions 登记/交易/托管/结算 (01 §1.1–§1.2).
- STEX boards; **技术授信 40.58 亿元**; 50 market outlets, 19 core network members, 8 regional centres, 32 countries; cumulative 429.92亿元 and 10,435 results at 26 July 2024 (01 §1.2; 02 §2.1).
- STEX annual transaction value 2021–2023: **30.47 / 101.15 / 189.22 亿元**, CAGR 149.20 % (01 §1.4; 02 §2.1; 03 §2; README).
- Financials (万元): STEX revenue 2,316.64 / 2,839.96 / 2,842.71 / 956.60 (H1 2024), net profit −1,374.85 / −1,187.42 / 625.25 / −1,985.86; NETC operating company revenue 3,185.16 / 3,505.09 / 4,620.17 / 384.31, net profit 265.55 / 172.18 / 175.30 / −2,422.67 (02 §4; 03 §2).
- Explanations: losses from R&D; revenue mostly in H2/Q4; subsidies recognised late (03 §2; 02 §4).
- Group: 杨科创 is STEX's largest shareholder and initiated and operates NETC; 谢吉华 is STEX chairman, NETC president and 杨科创 general manager; 颜明峰 is STEX president and 杨科创 deputy GM (01 Summary, §3).
- 上技贸易: STEX's wholly owned international arm in Lingang (01 §1.2).

## Excerpts

1. STEX transaction values
   > 2021 年-2023 年，上海技交所成交金额分别为 30.47 亿元、101.15 亿元以及189.22 亿元，成交金额年均复合增长率达 149.20%

   *Translation:* "From 2021 to 2023, STEX's transaction value was ¥3.047 billion, ¥10.115 billion and ¥18.922 billion, a compound annual growth rate of 149.20%"

2. Loss explanation
   > 2021 年及 2022 年净利润为负主要系研发费用较高所致

   *Translation:* "Net losses in 2021 and 2022 were mainly due to high R&D expenses"

3. STEX network and credit
   > 累计完成技术授信金额 40.58 亿元；赋能区域科创生态建设，累计链接全国 50 个技术市场网点，引入 19 家核心交易网络成员单位，成立 8 个区域创新中心核心节点。

   *Translation:* "…cumulative technology credit of ¥4.058 billion; it has linked 50 technology-market outlets nationwide, brought in 19 core trading-network members and set up 8 core nodes of regional innovation centres."

4. Group structure
   > 杨科创是上海技术交易所的最大股东，具有独立自持交易牌照的技术要素市场

   *Translation:* "杨科创 is the largest shareholder of STEX, a technology-factor market holding its own independent trading licence"

   > 杨科创发起并托管运营的国家级区域技术转移平台—国家技术转移东部中

   *Translation:* "The national regional technology-transfer platform initiated and operated under trust by 杨科创 — National Technology Transfer Center East [NETC]"

5. Leadership
   > 截至本招募说明书更新出具之日，运营管理统筹机构核心运营管理人员包括上海技术交易所董事长、国家技术转移东部中心总裁、杨科创总经理谢吉华；杨科创副总经理（主持工作）、上海技术交易所总裁颜明峰

   *Translation:* "As of the date of this updated prospectus, the core operating managers include 谢吉华, chairman of STEX, president of NETC and general manager of 杨科创; and 颜明峰, deputy general manager (in charge) of 杨科创 and president of STEX"

6. Who 杨科创 is
   > 原始权益人指上海杨浦科技创业中心有限公司（以下简称“杨科创”）。

   *Translation:* "The original equity holder means 上海杨浦科技创业中心有限公司 (Shanghai Yangpu Technology Entrepreneurship Center Co., Ltd., '杨科创')."

## Notes

- **Entity name (corrected during the parallel revision):** when this work started, 01 called the group "上海杨浦科技创新（集团）有限公司 (Yangpu Science & Technology Innovation Group, '杨科创')". In the prospectus **杨科创 is 上海杨浦科技创业中心有限公司**; 上海杨浦科技创新（集团）有限公司 is only one of its shareholders, nominating 1 of 5 directors ("上海杨浦科技创新（集团）有限公司提名 1 名董事"), while 上海市科技创业中心 nominates 3. The current 01 and README use 上海杨浦科技创业中心有限公司.
- **Ratio:** 02 Summary and 03 §1 use revenue / value = 28.43 / 18,922 ≈ 0.15 %; both inputs are in this prospectus.
