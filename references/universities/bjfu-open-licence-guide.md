# BJFU guide to patent open-licence declarations (2024)

## What it is

A university research office's slide-style guide to China's patent open-licensing system. It quotes the Patent Law (Arts. 50–51), the Implementing Rules and the Patent Examination Guidelines. The guide itself is a university document (excerpts only); the statutory passages it quotes are official texts.

## What the docs take from it

- Implementer gives **written notice** and **pays the published fee** → obtains a licence (Patent Law Art. 51) (09 §5).
- Open licence **not allowed** while an exclusive or sole licence is in force, when annual fees are unpaid, or when the patent is pledged without the pledgee's consent (09 §5).
- Annual-fee reduction during open licensing (context for 09 §5).

## Excerpts

1. Patent Law Art. 51 (as quoted in the guide)
   > 第五十一条 任何单位或者个人有意愿实施开放许可的专利的，以书面方式通知专利权人，并依照公告的许可使用费支付方式、标准支付许可使用费后，即获得专利实施许可。

   *Translation:* "Art. 51: Any entity or individual wishing to exploit a patent under open licence obtains a licence after notifying the patentee in writing and paying the licence fee in the announced manner and amount."

2. Exclusions (as quoted in the guide)
   > （1）专利权处于独占或者排他许可有效期限内的；

   *Translation:* "(1) the patent is within the term of an exclusive or sole licence;"

   > （3）没有按照规定缴纳年费的；

   *Translation:* "(3) the annual fees have not been paid as required;"

   > （4）专利权被质押，未经质权人同意的；

   *Translation:* "(4) the patent is pledged and the pledgee has not consented;"

## Notes

- The exclusions are in the Implementing Rules (Art. 86 in the guide's numbering) and the Patent Examination Guidelines, not in Patent Law Arts. 50–51. An earlier 09 §5 cited only "Patent Law Arts. 50–51"; the current 09 §5 and Sources cite "Implementing Regulations (2023) Art. 86" as well.
