# Fudan cash-reward (income-distribution) notice SYFP-2026-011

## What it is

A university notice publishing how licence and transfer income is distributed to inventors, issued under MOST's 2018 notice rules ([国科发政〔2018〕103号](../policy/most-2018-103-reward-notice.md)). University document; excerpts only. The attached distribution table (with inventors' names) is an image in the PDF and is not reproduced.

## What the docs take from it

- Real practice of a **15-working-day** reward notice (29 May – 18 June 2026 = 15 working days) with **real-name written objections** (09 §5 "Fudan practice").

## Excerpts

1. Legal basis
   > 根据国科发【2018】103 号文件，科技成果转化完成单位需对取得职务科技成果转化现金奖励相关信息予以公示。

   *Translation:* "Under document 国科发〔2018〕103号, units completing a transformation must publish information on cash rewards from service inventions."

2. Period and objections
   > 公示期限自 2026 年 5 月 29 日至 2026 年 6 月 18 日。

   *Translation:* "The notice runs from 29 May 2026 to 18 June 2026."

   > 公示期内对公示内容有异议的，以书面形式向科学技术研究院提出，需写出具体、真实的情况，以实名提出异议。

   *Translation:* "Objections during the notice period must be submitted in writing to the Institute of Science and Technology, stating specific and true facts, under the objector's real name."

## Notes

- The extractable text does not show technology-contract registration data; if present, it is in the image table. The requirement to include it comes from 国科发政〔2018〕103号 (item 3), not from this notice's text.
- The notice gives the contract value (¥17.5 million, instalments; first ¥2 million received).
