Yes. If you mean the **best-known / official Shanghai technology-transfer and technology-trading platform**, the main one is **上海技术交易所 (Shanghai Technology Exchange, STEX / 上技所)**.

It is particularly important because it is a **national-level technology trading market** and was jointly established by the former Ministry of Science and Technology and the Shanghai Municipal Government. It provides technology transaction, technology-transfer,成果转化, IP transactions, financing, and related services. [Shanxi Provincial Government](https://xyd.shyp.gov.cn/company-detail/10?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**Official site:** [上海技术交易所（Shanghai Technology Exchange）](http://www.stte.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

There is also another very important Shanghai platform you may be thinking of:

**技术转移东部中心（National Technology Transfer Center East） / NETC**  
[技术转移东部中心（NETC）](https://www.netcchina.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

NETC is more focused on **technology-transfer networks, technology matching, commercialization, international technology transfer, and connecting technology supply with industrial demand**. Shanghai government materials describe its technology-results database as containing tens of thousands of domestic/international technology results and a large patent/service-provider network. [技术转移东部中心](https://www.netcchina.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

### The distinction is roughly

| Platform                    | Main role                                                             |
| --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **上海技术交易所 (STEX)**   | **Technology trading / transaction marketplace**                      |
| **技术转移东部中心 (NETC)** | **Technology transfer, matching & commercialization network**         |
| 上海市科技创业中心          | Government innovation/startup & technology commercialization services |
| 上海国际技术交易市场        | International technology-transfer/trading activities                  |

If what you're looking for is a **Chinese online marketplace where universities/research institutes/companies publish technologies and companies can find, evaluate, and potentially acquire/license them**, I would start with **上海技术交易所 and NETC** rather than a generic technology website.
