### 3. But your question about **"most revenue for the platform"** is different

This is the important distinction.

I **cannot responsibly say that STEX has the highest platform revenue** based on the publicly available information.

Why?

Because:

> **transaction value ≠ platform revenue**

For example, STEX can facilitate ¥30.9B of technology transactions without earning ¥30.9B. Its revenue comes from things such as transaction services, certification, membership, professional services, financing-related services, etc.

Similarly, InnoMatch can facilitate billions of yuan of intended transactions without taking a large percentage of those transactions.

In fact, InnoMatch has historically experimented with relatively low-cost/free participation and incentive mechanisms. One report describes its technology-manager point system, where platform points could be redeemed for RMB, illustrating that the platform's objective has been ecosystem development rather than simply charging transaction commissions. [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/676377066_260616?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

I couldn't find sufficiently reliable public financial statements showing:

**STEX annual platform revenue vs. NETC/InnoMatch annual platform revenue**

so I would **not rank them by revenue without better financial data**.
